# Shared templates define the information-quality contract

Accepted 3 November 2026, reaffirming the owner's country, university and programme template decision.

Each level uses one shared template and a common, versioned data model; their information contract drives research and comparable quality for every institution. Every review addresses the same relevant questions with sourced facts or explicit, scoped gaps and rechecks, preserving real differences in programmes, intakes, campuses, qualifications and applicant routes. We accept the normalization and evidence work this requires: new providers map into the common schema, and a genuinely unrepresentable fact gets a documented shared model extension applied consistently, because institution-specific pages or arbitrary record shapes would make worldwide information parity harder to maintain.

The operational contract is in [PAGE_TEMPLATES.md](../PAGE_TEMPLATES.md) and the separate coverage layers in [PARITY.md](../research/schools/PARITY.md). Omitted sections remain research gaps where relevant; a shared shell alone does not establish complete information.
