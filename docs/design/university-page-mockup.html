Four things to know before you apply

Every university answers the same four questions. If one has no published answer, it says who to ask.

IB

What your IB needs

The IB Diploma counts as the Dutch VWO. There is no minimum points total, and an IB taught in English waives the English test.

Strictest subjects: Maths AA HL and Physics HL, for Physics, Astronomy and Applied Physics.

€

What it costs

€2,771 a year for EU and EEA students on most degrees (2027/28). Two selective degrees charge €5,343 (2026/27 figure).

Plus a €100 application fee, once a year.

→

How to apply

Apply in Studielink, then finish in the RUG Progress Portal. Apply between 1 October and 28 February to most degrees and you hear back within 15 working days.

!

Worth knowing

You must pass at least 45 of the 60 first-year credits to continue. Liberal Arts and Sciences and Global Politics and Sustainability ask for all 60.
