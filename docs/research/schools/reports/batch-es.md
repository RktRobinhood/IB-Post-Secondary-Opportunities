# Batch report: es-* (Spain, 13 institutions), issue #43

Researched 2026-09-26 in the user's Chrome (own tab; pages read as rendered text or same-origin fetch). One line per school.
Country route: IB holders need UNEDasiss accreditation (UNED, "se abre el 4 de febrero y se cierra el 1 de diciembre de cada año"); public universities then admit through their own or the region's preinscripción; for 2026/27 UNED gave "La fecha límite para solicitar la admisión en las universidades públicas por parte de los estudiantes internacionales ... es el 7 de julio de 2026" (https://unedasiss.uned.es/fechas_clave). No 2027 date there yet.

Publication boundary: list a programme only when the official page confirms a complete English route. A required language course, placement or final project may remain if it is disclosed on the card; a degree with substantial Spanish/Catalan teaching or an unverified bilingual label does not. This removes attractive but unusable false positives from the English-taught set.
