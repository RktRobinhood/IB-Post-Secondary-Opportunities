# Wave 3: United States and New Zealand (7 Oct 2026)

Method: university pages read as text (curl or browser tab for Cloudflare-walled Colby). Dates cross-checked against the Common App
"2026-27 First-year deadlines" grid (https://content.commonapp.org/Files/ReqGrid.pdf, updated 25 Sep 2026), which states the year; most
university pages give the date without a year, so the open 2026-27 cycle (entry autumn 2027) is assumed and the grid confirms it.
