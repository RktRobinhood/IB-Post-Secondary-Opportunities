## Hong Kong Shue Yan University — 29 October 2026 review

`hk-hksyu.json` is the earlier 7 October draft, retained unchanged. The official
[IB page](https://uao.hksyu.edu/en/qualifications/ib) now directly confirms its
24-point Diploma rule and the dated 2027 application table. However, the
[international admissions page](https://uao.hksyu.edu/en/student-admission/non-local_international)
requires programme-dependent Chinese competence, including Cantonese for some
courses. The [course list](https://uao.hksyu.edu/en/study) does not establish that
nearly the entire catalogue is delivered in English.

Keep the proposed `catalogue` scope held until programme-level delivery evidence
supports it or an exhaustive English-taught subset can be listed. In particular,
do not advertise its 15 June qualification-completion date as an ordinary final
IB results deadline for May-session students; the same page says 2027 IB results
arrive in July. Resolve the conditional-offer route first.

international brochure. This draft is preserved for the wider research queue;
