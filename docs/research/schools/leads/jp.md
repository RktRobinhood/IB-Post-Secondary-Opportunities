## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "No central portal — apply direct to each university" (https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/).

**No countrywide deadline. Two systems side by side:**

- **The Japanese-taught route** (the regular faculties): **EJU** (Examination for Japanese University
  Admission for International Students, run by JASSO) plus Japanese proficiency and each university's
  own exam; entry in **April**. Summary: 479 universities (over 60%), including most national
  universities, use EJU scores. **Not for this reader** unless they have Japanese.
- **The English-track route** (every institution below): **direct application**, document screening
  (transcripts, predicted IB grades, essays, English test, sometimes video or interview), **no EJU and
  no Japanese required** at entry. Entry is usually in **September or October**; some have April entry.

**The intake-year trap.** A student finishing the IB in May 2027 realistically enters
**September/October 2027**, applying **on predicted grades** in windows that **close before the IB
exams** — mostly **October 2026 to January 2027** (Kyoto iUP 2 Nov – 3 Dec 2026; Nagoya G30 round 1
2 Nov – 19 Nov 2026, round 2 5 – 21 Jan 2027; UTokyo College of Design video 13–16 Nov 2026; ICU
online application from January 2027; APU open from 1 September 2026 with rolling round deadlines —
all hints). Several windows are **already open** at the time of writing. **April entries are the wrong
cycle:** "April 2027" programmes (Sophia's new DGTech, Osaka's IUPS, Tohoku Gateway's April intake)
start before the IB results; the reader's April option is **April 2028**. Also:
- **Kyoto iUP** and **Hokkaido MJSP** enrol in **October 2027** into a **six-month intensive Japanese
  course**; the bachelor's itself starts in **April 2028** (hint) — say so.
- Kyushu and Nagoya enrol in **October**; Keio PEARL on **22 September 2027** (manifest); APU on
  21 September 2027 (hint).
