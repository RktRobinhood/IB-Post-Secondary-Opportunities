## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "DGES — Concurso Nacional de Acesso" (https://www.dges.gov.pt/).

This is the country where the **EU/non-EU split decides the route**, and the reader is mostly EU:

- **EU/EEA citizens are excluded from the International Student Statute** (hint below, from DGES). So
  the university pages titled "International students" / "Special Admission Contest for International
  Students" (Concurso Especial para Estudantes Internacionais) are **for non-EU applicants** and are the
  wrong route for most readers. Record them with `who` set accordingly, never as the EU route.
- **EU students at public universities** apply through the **Concurso Nacional de Acesso (CNA)**, one
  national application with national phases. A student with a foreign secondary diploma can ask to
  **substitute the Portuguese entrance exams ("provas de ingresso") with foreign exams**; the
  International Baccalaureate is on the list of systems for which this is allowed. So the countrywide
  deadline is the CNA's (with an earlier cut-off for substitution requests).
- **Private / Catholic universities** (Católica Lisbon, UCP Porto) and some programmes admit directly,
  with their own calendars and maths-exam requirements.
- DGES's functions have been integrated into the **Instituto para o Ensino Superior, I.P. (IES)** —
  dges.gov.pt URLs may move.

Hints to verify (from search summaries, NOT verified):
- CNA 2026 first phase: applications from 20 July; **until 29 July for candidates requesting
  substitution of entrance exams by foreign exams** (and priority contingents); until 6 August for
  others; results 23 August; enrolment 24–27 August; second phase 24 August–20 September. 2027 dates not
  seen.
- Systems eligible for exam substitution include the International Baccalaureate, European Schools,
  Cambridge International, France, UK, Switzerland, Canada, Belgium, Netherlands, Norway, Cape Verde.
  CNAES publishes the list of homologated foreign exams by 31 May each year. Exams valid five years.
- Documents: proof of the foreign course with final grade, exam grades, and an **equivalence certificate**
  with the final grade converted to the 0–200 scale.
- EU nationals and their family members are excluded from the International Student Statute; so are
  non-EU residents in Portugal for 2+ years.
