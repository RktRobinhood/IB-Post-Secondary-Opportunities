## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "UNEDasiss (accreditation) then each region's preinscripción system" (https://unedasiss.uned.es/).

- **Public universities: no countrywide application deadline.** An IB student first gets a UNEDasiss
  accreditation (which gives an admission grade), then pre-enrols through the **region**:
  - Madrid (UAM, UC3M, UCM and the other public universities): one "Distrito Único" application
    listing degrees in order of preference.
  - Catalonia (UB, UAB, UPF, UPC): one pre-enrolment on the Portal d'Accés a la Universitat.
  - Several Madrid public universities also run their own **"Early Admission"** route for international
    students (UC3M, UAM, UCM found), outside the regional pre-enrolment.
- **Private universities** (IE, ESADE, Navarra, Deusto, Comillas, CEU) admit directly, in rounds or on a
  rolling basis, with their own tests. Several still ask for the UNEDasiss credential at enrolment.
