## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "No shared application — six autonomous universities, six forms, six fees, six deadlines.
There is a shared acceptance: every offer you hold across them is settled once, in the Joint
Acceptance Portal" (https://www.nus.edu.sg/oam/admissions/important-dates).

**No countrywide deadline; per-university windows, but they cluster.** The academic year starts in
**August**, so a May-2027 IB student enters **August 2027 (AY2027/2028)** — the intake matches the
reader. Pattern from the summaries (hints):

- Autonomous universities open their international-qualification windows between **October/December
  and January**, and close between **February and March** (2026 cycle examples: NUS 3 Dec 2025 –
  23 Feb 2026; SUTD 2 Jan – 2 Mar 2026; SIT and DigiPen mid-Jan – 19 Mar 2026; SMU predicted scores
  by 31 Mar 2026; NTU "15 March"). **2027-cycle dates were not seen** except NUS's IB-specific ones
  below. Take each from the page for AY2027/2028.
- **Apply with school-attested predicted grades**; final results sent directly by the IB in July.
  Several require the applicant to **authorise the IB to release the transcript** (NUS, NTU, SUTD,
  LASALLE) and to enter the IB personal code by a date.
- **Interviews / tests** are common: SMU (shortlisted), SUTD (conversation session; possible
  "University Entrance Examination" for early consideration), SIT (admission interview), SUSS
  (interviews and selection tests), ESSEC (interview).
- **MOE Tuition Grant**: international students can apply; it cuts the fee but requires **working for a
  Singapore entity for three years** after graduating. Medicine/dentistry carry a longer Ministry of
  Health bond. This is a country rule — belongs on the country record.
- **Joint Acceptance Portal** (manifest): one acceptance across the autonomous universities' offers.

Traps:
- NTU's IB page summary mixed years ("May 2026 exam … closing date on 15 March … before July 2026");
  read the page itself. It also says May-exam candidates **cannot apply to LKCMedicine, Renaissance
  Engineering, and the Arts/Science (with Education) programmes** in that exercise (hint).
- NUS summaries showed dates from three cycles (AY2025/26 closing 5 Feb 2025; AY2026/27 closing
  23 Feb 2026; AY2027/28 IB deadlines). Only the last is this reader's.
- Private institutions (JCU, Curtin, SIM, DigiPen via SIT's portal) run trimester/multiple intakes
  with rolling admission.
