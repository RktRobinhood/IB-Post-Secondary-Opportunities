## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "University of Malta online application (eSIMS)" (https://www.um.edu.mt/courses).

**No national system; every institution admits directly, with its own deadline.** The University of
Malta is the de facto national university: apply online from November for the October intake (smaller
February intake), with the main deadline published on its "Dates and deadlines" page (2027 not yet
seen). The country record notes the UM treats the IB as a **local** qualification ("Local
Qualifications" on the form) and that HL = Advanced, SL = Intermediate, ab initio does not count.

The other institutions (MCAST, ITS, private colleges) require **MQRIC recognition** (the Malta
Qualifications Recognition Information Centre, part of MFHEA) or an EQF-level declaration for foreign
qualifications. Teaching is in English throughout, so English-taught lists = the whole bachelor's
catalogue; the UM is a `catalogue`-scope candidate.

Official system URLs found:
- "Recognition of an academic programme by the Malta Qualifications Recognition Information Centre (MQRIC)" https://www.servizz.gov.mt/en/services/web-00527
- "Academic Qualifications - Malta Further & Higher Education Authority" https://mfhea.mt/academic-qualifications/
- "Academic Qualifications- Apply Here - MFHEA" https://mfhea.mt/academic-qualifications-apply-here/
- "Malta Qualifications Registry" https://qualifications.mfhea.gov.mt/ (check that a private provider's degree is accredited)

Hints to verify (NOT verified): nothing IB-specific from MQRIC surfaced.
