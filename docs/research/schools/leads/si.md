## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "eVS (eVS Prijava), with SI-PASS login" (https://portal.evs.gov.si/prijava/?locale=en).

**A countrywide deadline.** One national **joint call for enrolment** (skupni razpis za vpis), published
around the end of January/February, covers the public universities (Ljubljana, Maribor, Primorska, Nova
Gorica, Novo Mesto, New University) and sets the same application periods for all; applications go
through **eVŠ**. 2026/27 pattern: **first period 18 February–18 March 2026**, second period 20–27 August
2026 (EU/Slovene), non-EU separate periods (e.g. 2–3 September 2026 at Ljubljana). 2027/28 call not yet
published (the country record expects late January 2027).

**EU citizens apply in the same round as Slovenians** ("European Union member countries citizens may
apply for enrolments that are intended for the citizens of the Republic of Slovenia"), and **pay no
tuition for full-time study** at public/concession programmes — but English-taught implementations can
carry a fee for everyone (Ljubljana EF €3,400, Maribor "mandatory for all candidates, regardless of
nationality"). Check per programme.

**The IB**: Ljubljana says the IB Diploma "is equivalent to the matura certificate in the Republic of
Slovenia and therefore does not require a special recognition procedure" (hint below).

Private institutions (Alma Mater Europaea, GEA College, ISSBS/MFDPŠ) may be in the joint call for
concession places or run their own calls — check each.
