## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "No central portal. The UAE has four admissions environments … federal institutions, UAE
private universities, and branch campuses of foreign universities — and, on its own, NYU Abu Dhabi,
which admits through NYU in New York on the American calendar"
(https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/education/higher-education/Higher-education-regulatory-bodies).

**No countrywide deadline; per-institution, in four patterns:**

1. **NYU Abu Dhabi — the US calendar.** Common App (NYU's), **Early Decision I, Early Decision II,
   Regular Decision (hint: RD deadline 5 January, decisions 1 April)**; Candidate Weekend selection;
   need-based aid for all nationalities via CSS Profile. Record its dates from NYUAD's "Key Dates and
   Deadlines" page for the 2027 entry cycle.
2. **Branch campuses of UK/Australian/US universities** (Heriot-Watt, Birmingham, Wollongong,
   Middlesex, RIT): direct application to the Dubai campus (Birmingham: **not through UCAS**, and it
   does not count as a UCAS choice — hint), home-university IB entry rules (Birmingham 32 points with
   HL grades; Heriot-Watt 24–28 by programme; UOWD 25), usually **rolling** with September and
   January/February intakes.
3. **UAE private universities** (AUS, AUD, CUD, SP Jain): American/Canadian-style rolling or
   term-based deadlines (AUS publishes "Deadlines and Important Dates"; AUD early applications to
   March/April, late to July/August — hint; CUD all year).
4. **Federal universities** (Khalifa, UAEU, Zayed): Ministry-regulated, primarily for UAE nationals;
   international places exist with their own criteria and fees (ZU international tuition AED 80,000 a
   year — hint).

- **Intake**: the academic year starts in **late August / September**, so a May-2027 IB student
  enters **Fall 2027** — the right intake. Several also admit in January (Spring 2028).
- **Certificate equivalency**: federal and some private institutions require an **equivalency of the
  Grade-12 certificate from the UAE Ministry of Education** for foreign curricula (ZU says so
  explicitly — hint). A Ministry admissions-criteria summary gives the IB threshold as **28 points
  with at least 4 in each of six subjects** (hint; from a mohesr.gov.ae PDF). Check whether this
  applies to branch campuses and NYUAD (probably not).
- **English tests**: many ask IELTS/TOEFL/EmSAT even of IB students unless English A is taken
  (Khalifa: IELTS 6.0 required of international applicants — hint).
- **Residence visa** sponsored by the university for international students (not searched).

Official system URLs found:
- "Equalising high school certificates | The Official Platform of the UAE Government" https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/education/school-education-k-12/equalising-high-school-certificates
- "Equivalency of General Education certificate in the UAE (Grade 12)" https://www.moe.gov.ae/En/EServices/ServiceCard/pages/certequivalent.aspx
- "Required documents to apply for certificate equivalency" https://www.moe.gov.ae/En/EServices/ServiceCard/Equ/Pages/RequiredDocs.aspx

- PDF "Annex 1: Entry criteria for Foundation and Bachelor level 2025- 2026" https://www.mohesr.gov.ae/En/EServices/ServiceCard/Napo/Documents/Admission%20Criteria/SUAD%20Entry%20criteria.pdf
