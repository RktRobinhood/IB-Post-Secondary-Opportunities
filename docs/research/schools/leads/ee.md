## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "DreamApply Estonia — one interface shared by nine institutions, but each sets its own
deadlines and decisions" (https://estonia.dreamapply.com/).

**One shared application interface, but no countrywide deadline.** Each institution sets its own
dates, and several split EU/EEA from non-EU. 2026-cycle hints: Tartu bachelor's to 15 April (English
test results by then too); TalTech opened 1 February; EMU Veterinary 1 June EU / 4 May non-EU; EUAS
30 June non-EU; EBS interviews 6 August; Tallinn University 1 November–1 March (country record, 2027
entry). The national list of English-taught bachelor's at studyinestonia.ee is the completeness check.
