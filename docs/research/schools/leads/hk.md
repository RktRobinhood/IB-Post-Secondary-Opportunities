## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "No central portal for non-JUPAS applicants — apply direct to each university"
(https://admissions.hku.hk/international).

**No countrywide system for an overseas IB student.** JUPAS is only for Hong Kong's local HKDSE
students; IB students (even in Hong Kong) apply **direct** through each university's
"International / Non-JUPAS" scheme. Each sets its own rounds, which cluster:

- Applications open **September/October 2026** for **September 2027** entry (the right intake:
  a May-2027 IB student enters in September 2027). HKAPA's 2027/28 applications opened
  7 September 2026 (hint).
- **Early / priority round** in **November** (HKUST priority deadline **26 November 2026** — hint)
  and a **regular round** in **January** (CUHK regular round for non-local applicants
  **7 January 2027** — hint); many then continue **rolling** until places fill (HKUST system
  closes 30 June 2027 — hint).
- **Predicted grades** from the IB coordinator; final IB results sent by the IB (several give their
  IB institution code: HKBU 000247, HKSYU 010755). Interviews for shortlisted applicants
  (HKU Nov–Jan; HKMU, THEi, HKAPA auditions Dec 2026–May 2027).
- **IB minimum** commonly 24 points (PolyU: 24 with grade 4 in two HL subjects; HSUHK: 24 with 12+
  from HL; HKSYU 24) — admitted students score much higher (PolyU says admitted IB students typically
  score 31–36, hint). **Advanced standing** for strong IB results at CUHK, PolyU, CityU.
- **Non-local tuition** is a single high rate (CUHK HK$230,000 for 2027-28 entry; HKUST HK$215,000 —
  hints). EU/EEA students count as non-local; a student visa is needed.

Traps:
- "Non-JUPAS" pages at several universities are for **local** applicants with other qualifications;
  use the **international / non-local** page for this reader (CUHK, Lingnan and HKBU each have both).
- Several fee/deadline pages are for **2026 entry** (CUHK "Information Guide 2026 Entry", HKBU "2026"
  PDF). CUHK's 2027-28 fee and HKUST's 2027 dates were seen for the right cycle.
