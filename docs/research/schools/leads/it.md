## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "Universitaly (Ministry portal; pre-enrolment is for visa applicants only)" (https://www.universitaly.it/).

- **No countrywide application deadline.** Each university runs its own calls ("bandi"), usually in
  several rounds, most now on an `apply.<uni>.it` platform for international qualifications. EU/EEA
  applicants apply straight to the university; **Universitaly pre-enrolment is for non-EU applicants
  who need a visa**.
- **Tests**: many programmes use CISIA's TOLC family (TOLC-E economics, TOLC-I engineering, English
  TOLC, CEnT-S in English); several accept the SAT instead; the private universities (Bocconi, Luiss,
  Cattolica) run their own tests or accept SAT/ACT/IB. Politecnico di Torino has its own TIL.
- **IB recognition** is set by the Ministry's annual rules for foreign qualifications: the IB Diploma is
  valid if the student entered the IB after being promoted to the penultimate school year; 12 years of
  schooling in total (see Polimi, Bocconi hints below).
- **Fees** at public universities are income-based (ISEE for Italian income; a fixed amount by country
  for foreign-income families); private universities charge flat or banded fees.
