## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "Study in Lithuania (national information portal; applications go direct to each
university)" (https://studyin.lt/). **This is incomplete, and it is the main trap in this country.**

There are **two routes**, and for an EU/EEA citizen the national one may be compulsory at some schools:

- **LAMA BPO** (the Association of Lithuanian Higher Education Institutions for Organising Joint
  Admission) — the national joint admission system. Main admission **June–July**, supplementary in
  **August** (countrywide dates, set each year). It handles **state-funded places**, and a Vilnius
  University summary confirms EU citizens "can apply for both state-funded and non-state-funded study
  places". Foreign-diploma holders need **SKVC recognition** (or recognition by an authorised university)
  and SKVC grade conversion; "IB diploma holders must only submit the academic recognition decision to
  LAMA BPO" (hint).
- **University portals** (apply.vu.lt, apply.ktu.edu, apply.mruni.eu, apply.ku.lt, apply.vda.lt, …) —
  for fee-paying international applicants, with **per-university deadlines, split by citizenship**
  (EU/EEA later: VU 1 July, MRU 24 July 2026, Kauno kolegija 1 June; non-EU earlier).

**At the Vilnius Academy of Arts the page says EU/EEA citizens must apply via LAMA BPO, not the
university portal** (hint below). Check, per institution, which route an EU citizen uses for an
English-taught programme — the answer changes the deadline from the university's to LAMA BPO's.
