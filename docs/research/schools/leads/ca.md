## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest portal: none. **There is no national system; it is provincial.**

- **Ontario (10 here: U of T, Waterloo, Queen's, McMaster, Western, uOttawa, York, TMU, Carleton,
  Guelph) — OUAC.** Applicants from outside Ontario (including all international applicants) use the
  **OUAC 105** form. Hints: OUAC's deadline to submit for September 2027 is **15 January 2027**, and
  there is an **earlier OUAC date, 1 December 2026, for international students who need a study permit
  and a Provincial Attestation Letter** — that is every EU/EEA student. This needs checking on OUAC's
  own dates page before any record carries it. Each university then sets its own document and
  supplementary deadlines (Waterloo Engineering AIF 1 Feb 2027; Queen's recommends applying by 20 Nov;
  Carleton's international main deadline 1 April 2027; uOttawa many programmes open to May), so the
  OUAC date is not the whole story.
- **British Columbia (UBC, SFU, UVic)** — direct to each university (UBC deadline ~15 January; UVic
  31 January; SFU per term). BC domestic students use EducationPlannerBC; check whether it applies to
  international applicants.
- **Quebec (McGill, Concordia)** — direct; McGill ~15 January for schools outside Canada/US,
  Concordia 1 March (1 February recommended for international).
- **Alberta, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Nova Scotia (UAlberta, UCalgary, Manitoba, USask, Dal)** —
  direct; Calgary opens 15 August for international applicants and closes 1 March; Dal opens
  1 October.
- **IB is an entry requirement here**, stated in IB terms by most (e.g. UBC 24 incl. bonus; Queen's 28
  excl. bonus, no mark below 3; Western low-to-mid 30s competitive; McGill 5+ in every subject; Dal 26;
  Manitoba 24; Carleton 28; Guelph 28; uOttawa 29; Concordia 29). **Predicted grades** drive the offer;
  final results confirm it. Many also give **transfer credit** for HL 5+ (and a full year at Calgary,
  SFU, UVic for the Diploma).
- **Study permit and PAL**: EU/EEA citizens need a Canadian study permit, and since 2024 most
  undergraduate study permits need a **Provincial/Territorial Attestation Letter**, subject to an annual
  national cap. This affects every Canadian record's timeline (hints; see the IRCC page below).
