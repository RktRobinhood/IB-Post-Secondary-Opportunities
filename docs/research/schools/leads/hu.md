## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "For English-taught programmes you apply directly to each university, each with its own
portal. The central felvi.hu / E-felvételi system covers Hungarian-language and state-funded places
only." (https://www.felvi.hu/)

**Two systems, two kinds of deadline:**

- **English-taught, self-funded**: direct to each university, usually on a DreamApply-style portal
  (apply.u-szeged.hu, apply.elte.hu, apply.pte.hu, apply.mome.hu, apply.metropolitan.hu,
  e-admission.bme.hu, SEMAPHOR at Semmelweis). **Per-university deadlines**, mostly 15 May–30 June for
  September, often with earlier dates for visa-needing (non-EU) applicants; medical schools run rolling
  online entrance exams from March. Some universities (Corvinus: 2 February for 2026; CEU: four rounds
  from October) close much earlier.
- **Felvi (E-felvételi)**: the national system for Hungarian-language and state-funded places, with
  one **countrywide deadline** (15 February in recent cycles, per a MOME summary; check the 2027 date).
  EU citizens are treated like Hungarian citizens for these places — so a Hungarian-speaking IB student
  goes through Felvi, and at MOME and the Liszt Academy EU citizens appear to have a separate
  procedure/fee from non-EU (see those entries).
- **Stipendium Hungaricum is closed to EU citizens.** University pages mixing SH rules (e.g. Szeged
  medicine "no IB exemption for SH applicants") are not the EU route.
