## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "None. The official national portal states there is no single central application form —
each institution, and usually each faculty, runs its own." (https://studyin.gov.cz/study-programmes/)

**No countrywide deadline, and often not even a university-wide one: the faculty sets it.** Seen in
2026-cycle summaries: Charles Faculty of Science 28 February, Charles Maths & Physics 30 April; UCT
1 December–31 January; CZU 15 September–31 December (second round to March/April); VSE 30 April (some
programmes a third intake to 10 June); Brno FEEC 1 April–31 May; West Bohemia 31 July; AAU rolling.
Every faculty's e-application (e-přihláška) is separate, with its own fee.

**Recognition of the secondary diploma** is the Czech-specific step. Two routes: general
**nostrification** by a regional authority (Prague City Hall for Prague), valid everywhere, slow (up to
~3 months) — or **recognition by the faculty inside the admission procedure**, valid only for that
faculty. Charles University says that since 1 March 2025 IB diplomas are excluded from the documents
assessed for a fee and are "automatically accepted" (hint below) — check whether that is a national
rule (Higher Education Act amendment) or Charles-only.

(Slovakia, Hungary, Germany, Poland, Slovenia) skip recognition (Masaryk summary).
