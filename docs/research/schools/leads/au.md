## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "UAC for New South Wales and the ACT, where an IB Diploma makes you eligible; direct to the
university everywhere else" (https://www.uac.edu.au/future-applicants/international-year-12-students).

**No national system: state tertiary admission centres, plus direct applications.** What the searches
suggest, for an IB student at a school **outside Australia** (the reader):

- **NSW/ACT — UAC** (Sydney, UNSW, UTS, Macquarie, Wollongong, ANU). UAC says international students
  taking the IB Diploma can apply through it (up to six preferences; early-bird fee to 30 September;
  IB results to UAC by the IB; IBAS emailed from 18 December 2026; most semester 1, 2027 offers in
  December–January). ANU *encourages* IB students "in Australia and overseas" to use UAC. **But
  Sydney's own page says most international students apply direct, and only IB students in Australia
  must use UAC.** So for Sydney-region universities both routes may exist — check each university's
  page and record what it says, without picking one for it.
- **Victoria — VTAC** (Melbourne, Monash, RMIT, Deakin): the universities' pages say VTAC is for IB
  students **in Australia or New Zealand**; everyone else applies **direct**. RMIT: IB completed before
  12 January 2027 may use VTAC; after VTAC's January Round 2 (27 January 2027) all apply direct.
- **Queensland — QTAC** (UQ, QUT, Griffith): QTAC for Australian Year 12 / IB in Australia; others
  direct (UQ: offers for semester 1, 2027 run 1 October 2026 – 16 February 2027).
- **WA — TISC** (UWA, Curtin) and **SA — SATAC** (Adelaide University): not searched; the university
  pages found point to direct international applications.
- **Tasmania (UTAS)**: direct (not searched).
- **IB is an entry-score requirement here**, converted to an ATAR-equivalent / selection rank (UAC's
  IBAS table in NSW/ACT; each university's own table elsewhere). Several publish per-course **minimum
  and guaranteed IB scores** for international students (Melbourne, UNSW's 2027 entry table, QUT's
  IB cut-offs, UTS 28 final / 33 predicted, UWA 24 minimum). Predicted grades accepted by most, with
  buffers (UNSW +2).

Official system URLs found:
- "International Year 12 students - UAC" https://uac.edu.au/future-applicants/international-year-12-students
- "International applications and offers - UAC" https://uac.edu.au/current-applicants/international-applications-and-offers
- "IBAS and ATAR Equivalent for IB Students | UAC" https://uac.edu.au/future-applicants/admission-criteria/ib-applicants
- PDF "Undergraduate applications for 2026–2027 Key dates for Year 12 students" https://uac.edu.au/assets/documents/ug-fact-sheets/ug-key-dates-year12.pdf
- VTAC, QTAC, TISC, SATAC: **not searched** (names only; find their international-applicant pages).

Traps:
- **The intake year.** Australia's academic year starts in **February/March**. A Danish IB student
  finishing in May 2027 gets results in July 2027; "autumn 2027 entry" in Australian terms is the
  **mid-year (July) 2027** intake at best, and more realistically **semester 1, 2028**. UAC's
  IBAS-in-December timeline is for the **November** IB session (southern hemisphere). Records must say
  which intake a date belongs to; don't give a May-session student the semester 1, 2027 dates as theirs.
- Adelaide University is the new merged university (from January 2026); old University of Adelaide
  pages remain on the same domain.
- Several fee figures in summaries were for research degrees or online master's — ignore.
- UOW's "20 March 2027 (Europe/UK/Latin America)" may be a study-abroad deadline.
