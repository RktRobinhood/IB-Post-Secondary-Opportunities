## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "No central portal for the international track — apply direct to each university. Study in
Korea (NIIED) is the official information portal" (https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/en/main.do).

**No countrywide deadline; each university runs its own "international student" admission, usually
twice a year (spring and fall).** Pattern (hints):

- **Eligibility**: the international-student track ("Foreigners special admission") is for
  applicants who **and both of whose parents** are non-Korean nationals (Yonsei UIC: a student or
  parent holding dual Korean citizenship is not eligible). A Danish/EU student with no Korean parent
  qualifies.
- **Documents**: diploma and transcript **apostilled** (or consular-certified); passport copies of
  the student and both parents and proof of the relationship. IB results sent by the IB.
- **Language**: English-track applicants show English (TOEFL/IELTS or English-medium schooling);
  Korean-taught majors need TOPIK (not this reader's route). Ewha: TOPIK 4 before graduation is
  waived for the Division of International Studies (hint).
- **Visa**: D-2 student visa after admission.

**Intake-year trap.** The Korean year starts in **March** (spring); fall starts in **September**.
A May-2027 IB student realistically enters **Fall 2027 (September 2027)** — or **Spring 2028**.
Deadlines for Fall 2027 mostly fall between **late 2026 and spring 2027** (POSTECH: applications
expected March–April 2027; Yonsei UIC fall entrants hear in late June; Handong's document deadline
31 August for fall). Pages seen now are often for the **wrong intake**: **KAIST "2027 Spring" applications
18 Aug – 1 Sep 2026**, **SNU "2027 Spring" 6 – 9 July 2026** — both are March 2027 entries, before
the IB exams; don't record them as this reader's dates. Most Fall 2027 guides were not yet
published (Korea, SKKU, CAU, Ewha, Hanyang show Fall 2026 guides); UNIST's Fall 2027 guideline
exists (manifest; search confirmed the page).
