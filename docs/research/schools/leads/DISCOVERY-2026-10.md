## Recommendation

1. Add first: Bard College Berlin, John Cabot University, Saxion (plus Inholland/Avans/Windesheim as one Dutch batch), Hanken, and the Ivy/peer and UK Russell Group lists; these are what counsellors ask for and cost one record each.
2. Then the new countries with a real EU-fee story: AUBG (Bulgaria), RIT Croatia, BISLA (Slovakia), Babes-Bolyai (Romania), UNIC (Cyprus), and Monash/Nottingham Malaysia plus Georgetown/CMU Qatar as branch-campus entries.
3. Leave Ukraine, Latin America, Serbia and Liechtenstein out unless a browser session confirms English-taught bachelors; treat Israel, Georgia and Turkey as low-priority, travel-advice-dependent.
