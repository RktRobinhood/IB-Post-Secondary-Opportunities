## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "Study in Greece (@SiG application platform) and the Ministry's programme finder"
(https://apply.studyingreece.edu.gr/).

**Two unrelated routes, two kinds of deadline:**

- **English-taught programmes at public universities** (a handful: Athens archaeology BA and MD, AUTh's
  set, Crete and Patras medicine, AUEB IBT, Aegean on Rhodes) are fee-paying and **admit directly, each
  with its own deadline** — some very early (Crete Medicine early round in **November**, regular in
  March; Athens BA March–May; Patras Medicine June). Many are open **only to foreign nationals who did
  the last two school years abroad** — an EU (non-Greek) IB student at a school in Denmark fits; a Greek
  citizen may not. Several list on the national **AtSiG** platform.
- **Greek-taught public places** go through the **Ministry of Education's foreign-nationals category**
  (αλλοδαποί-αλλογενείς: candidates of non-Greek origin who graduated from a school abroad), with a
  **countrywide window of about eight days in early July** on the Ministry's platform
  (exams-foreigner.it.minedu.gov.gr) and a courier deadline the day after (country record). Greek
  language certification is then needed. Universities with no English bachelor's (NTUA, IHU, Ioannina,
  ASFA, Piraeus) are reachable only this way.
- **Private colleges** (ACG/Deree) admit directly, rolling.
