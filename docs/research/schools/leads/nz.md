## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "None. Universities New Zealand states that applications should be made direct to the
universities" (https://www.universitiesnz.ac.nz/international-students/next-steps).

**No national system, no countrywide deadline: apply direct to each university**, each with its own
dates. What the searches suggest:

- **IB Diploma = University Entrance equivalent.** Every university's IB page seen gives the same
  pattern (hints): **full Diploma, 24 points minimum**, plus "literacy" (English as Language A, SL or
  HL, or Language B HL) and "numeracy" (any maths subject). Programmes with limited places select
  above that (Massey's "preferential entry score" 26; Canterbury's international engineering route
  asks HL 6 in Chemistry, Physics and Maths). A non-native English speaker whose Diploma doesn't carry
  English A may need IELTS 6.0 (no band below 5.5) (hints).
- **Per-university dates**, typically "apply by X for semester 1 (February)" with late applications
  considered if places remain. Otago hint: international application period **1 May – 15 November**
  for semester 1 (February). Auckland publishes an "Undergraduate application closing dates" page
  (the 2026 closing date for semester one seen in summaries was 8 December 2025 — 2027/2028 not seen).
- **Predicted grades:** provisional/conditional offers on predicted results (AUT, Waikato hints).
- **Student visa** needed for EU/EEA citizens; universities advise applying 3–6 months ahead.

Official system URLs found:
- "Next steps | Universities New Zealand - Te Pōkai Tara" https://www.universitiesnz.ac.nz/international-students/next-steps
- "Am I eligible? | Universities New Zealand - Te Pōkai Tara" https://www.universitiesnz.ac.nz/international-students/am-i-eligible
- "For international students | Universities New Zealand - Te Pōkai Tara" https://www.universitiesnz.ac.nz/international-students

Traps:
- **The intake year (southern hemisphere).** The NZ academic year runs **February/March to November**,
  with a second intake in **July**. A student finishing the IB in May 2027 gets results in early July
  2027. Realistically they enter **Semester 1, 2028 (late February / early March 2028)**; Semester 2,
  2027 (mid-July 2027) is only possible for programmes with a July intake and with an offer on predicted
  grades, and it is a mid-year start (not every programme allows it; Unitec's July intake is domestic
  part-time only, per summary). "2027 closing dates" on NZ pages are for **February 2027** starts —
  the wrong cycle for this reader; the right one is the **semester one 2028** closing date (late
  2027), which is not published yet.
- Most fees pages seen are for **2026**.
- Massey's old `massey.ac.nz/massey/...cfm` URLs are an older site (the manifest's own IB link is one).
