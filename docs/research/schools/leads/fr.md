## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "Parcoursup" (https://www.parcoursup.gouv.fr/).

- **Public universities (licence year 1): one countrywide calendar.** EU/EEA/Swiss (and Monaco/Andorra)
  nationals apply on **Parcoursup** whatever their diploma, so an EU IB student uses Parcoursup. Non-EU
  nationals with a foreign diploma use the **DAP** (demande d'admission préalable) — the "Études en
  France" platform in some countries, or the paper "dossier blanc" elsewhere.
- **Grandes écoles and private schools** (Sciences Po, Polytechnique's Bachelor, ESCP, ESSEC, EDHEC,
  emlyon, AUP) run **their own international application** for foreign-diploma holders, with rounds.
  Some are also on Parcoursup for French-bac holders; Polytechnique says a candidate may apply via only
  one of the two routes in a year.
- **Fees**: national registration fee at public universities for EU/EEA students (set by ministerial
  order each year), plus CVEC; "differentiated" higher fees for non-EU students, with exemptions.
