## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "No central portal … Campus China (China Scholarship Council) … its domain no longer
resolves; joint-venture universities use the Common Application or their own portals, and the
Chinese universities publish inside login-gated application systems of their own"
(https://www.commonapp.org/).

**No countrywide deadline. Two systems:**

1. **Sino-foreign universities** follow their foreign partner's calendar:
   - **NYU Shanghai** — NYU's Common App; **Early Decision I (1 November)**, ED II, Regular Decision
     (hints: ED II decisions 14 February, RD 1 April).
   - **DKU** — Common App; applications for 2027 entry accepted from August 2026; ED / RD (old hint:
     CSS Profile 15 November ED, 1 February RD).
   - **XJTLU, UNNC, CUHK-SZ, BNBU, WKU** — own online systems, rolling or round-based, often open until
     spring/summer (UNNC 2026 cycle closed **21 June 2026**, results by 15 July 2026; CUHK-SZ system
     open **September to April**, English test by **28 May 2027** — hints). IB thresholds: CUHK-SZ
     30/45 with English 4+; XJTLU 30 (for Year 2 direct entry — check what Year 1 asks); UNNC
     typically 28–34.
2. **Chinese universities** — each university's International Students Office (ISO) and its own
   application system; deadlines typically in the spring before September entry; scholarships via
   CSC (Chinese Government Scholarship, through embassies), Shanghai Government and university
   scholarships.
   - **New national test: CSCA (China Scholastic Competency Assessment).** From the **2026/2027
     academic year**, applicants to **bachelor's degree** study in China must take the CSCA before
     applying (hint; organised by the China Scholarship Council). Sittings five times a year —
     January, March, April, June, December; **January and March sittings for Europe and the
     Americas** (hint). ZJU says all international undergraduate applicants must provide a valid
     CSCA report from 2026/27 (hint). **Check whether it applies to the Sino-foreign universities**
     (not seen) — this decides whether an EU IB student needs it for NYU Shanghai, XJTLU etc.
   - Some also set their own tests (PKU: a written entrance examination, or SAT 1400 + three APs for
     the no-exam route — an IB route not seen).

**Intake**: September. A May-2027 IB student enters **September 2027**; the right deadlines are the
**2027-entry** ones (autumn 2026 – spring 2027). Most Chinese-university pages seen are **2026**
guides (Tongji, SYSU, ZJU "2026" guide, ZJE closing 31 May 2026) — wrong cycle.

Official URLs found:
- Chinese Embassy (Mongolia) "Notice About the China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA)" https://mn.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/zytz/202510/t20251030_11743700.htm (an official notice, but for Mongolia; find the CSC's own CSCA site or a European embassy's notice before citing)
