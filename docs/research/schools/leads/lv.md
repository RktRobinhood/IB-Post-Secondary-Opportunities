## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "Study in Latvia (national information portal; applications go direct to each university)"
(https://studyinlatvia.lv/).

**No countrywide deadline for international applicants.** Each university runs its own portal (most are
DreamApply-style: apply.lu.lv, apply.rtu.lv, apply.lbtu.lv, apply.venta.lv, admission.sseriga.edu) and
sets **deadlines by citizenship band**: typically the latest for EU/EEA/Swiss citizens (e.g. UL 21 July
2025, RTU 1 August 2025, Ventspils 30 July), earlier for visa-free and visa-needing countries, earliest
for "additional assessment" countries (Cabinet Regulation No. 554). SSE Riga is the exception: one early
deadline (6 April 2027) for everyone.

**Recognition**: a foreign secondary certificate must be recognised by the **Academic Information
Centre (AIC)**; usually the university's admissions office sends the documents itself. Statement within
a month, extendable to 4 months (country record: EUR 54.60). Latvian residents apply through the national
"unified admissions" for Latvian-taught places — not the route for an English-taught IB applicant.

- "Academic Information Centre" https://aic.lv/en/
