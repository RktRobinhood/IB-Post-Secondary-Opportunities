## Country: how an IB student applies

Manifest: "None nationally. Almost every university runs its own IRK system" (https://study.gov.pl/how-to-apply).

**No countrywide deadline.** Each university runs its own online recruitment (IRK / e-Rekrutacja / ISR)
and its own timetable, fixed by a Senate admission resolution. Two patterns to keep apart:

- **English-taught programmes for foreigners** often have their own earlier or rolling schedule
  (Kozminski from March; SGH late June–July; medical universities spring to mid-July).
- **"On the same terms as Polish citizens"**: EU citizens can usually apply in the main (Polish)
  recruitment round, which runs June–July, and in some cases get free tuition (Polish-language study,
  and some English programmes: see the country record). Several "for foreigners" pages are the
  **fee-paying** route; check which one an EU citizen belongs to at each university (Wroclaw Tech,
  Warsaw Tech and AGH all split their pages this way).
- **The IB is recognised by law** (no NAWA recognition statement; no apostille at several
  universities) and at several technical universities an IB holder is **exempt from the foreigners'
  maths entrance exam** (Wroclaw Tech, Warsaw Tech, Wroclaw University). HL vs SL drives the score
  conversion.

Official system URLs found:
- "A Step-by-Step Guide on how to start your studies in Poland | study.gov.pl" https://study.gov.pl/how-to-apply
- "Admission requirements | study.gov.pl" https://study.gov.pl/admission-requirements
- "STUDYFINDER* | study.gov.pl" https://study.gov.pl/studyfinder
- "Foreign Students - NAWA" https://nawa.gov.pl/en/students/foreign-students

Hints to verify (NOT verified): study.gov.pl says campaigns usually start in April or May and may last
up to six months, and that candidates apply directly to each institution. Since 1 July 2025 foreign
certificates need a NAWA recognition statement, **except** documents from EU/EFTA/OECD states and IB/EB
diplomas (per SGH and Wroclaw Tech summaries). Only one institution (Poznan Medical) had published
2027 dates when the country record was built.
