# nl-uu — Utrecht University (retrieved 2026-09-25)

- Scope listed, 10 programmes (UCU and UCR excluded; they have their own records, pointed to in a note).
- Hand-off https://www.uu.nl/en/bachelors/bachelorsprogrammes: English site list, bachelor's only, "12 results" (the 10 programmes + UCR + UCU); its language filter shows English 12, Dutch 1 (Linguistics, bilingual).
- Dutch-site list https://www.uu.nl/bachelors/opleidingen: 54 bachelor's; labelled EN or NL/EN: the 12 above plus Economics and Mathematics (NL, EN) and Economie en bedrijfseconomie (= EBE).
- Excluded, Economics and Mathematics (BSc, uu.nl/bachelors/economics-and-mathematics): "Nederlands, Engels"; the mathematics half is taught in Dutch and there is no English page. Not in the national English leads either.
- Excluded, Studiekeuze123 "English variant" leads whose UU pages say Voertaal "Nederlands" with no English track: Aardwetenschappen (Earth Sciences), Liberal Arts and Sciences (UU's own, Dutch), Natuurwetenschap en innovatiemanagement, Culturele antropologie, Sociale geografie en planologie. Spaanse taal en cultuur excluded: taught in Dutch and Spanish ("NL, ES").
- Included but missing from national sources: History (BA, EN) is on UU's English list but in neither feed. Studiekeuze123's English-variant list also lacks College of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Literary Studies and Media and Culture (all in Study in NL); Linguistics appears there as "Taalwetenschap".
- Linguistics included: study-programme page, "In your first year ... taught entirely in English from day one"; 'Language and cognition' and 'Language and computation' "fully taught in English"; 'Taal en samenleving' "fully taught in Dutch". Programme ib says so.
- Tuition: UU programme pages print "Dutch and other EU/EEA students (statutory fee 2027-2028, full-time): € 2,771", so programmes carry €2,771 (2027/28), not the brief's €2,694 (2026/27). PPE is higher: "Dutch and other EU/EEA students 2027-2028: € 5,542". Coordinator may want to harmonise the statutory figure across NL records (rijksoverheid.nl still shows only "2026-2027 € 2.694"; DUB, UU's news site, reports the 2027 rise to €2,771).
- Official pages disagree on PPE's deadline: PPE's own page, "The absolute latest date for applications AND application files to be received is 14 January 2027, 23:59 hrs CET"; the limited-enrolment page gives Studielink "between 1 October 2026 and 15 January 2027 (23.59 CET)". Recorded closes 2027-01-14 (the route's own page).
- Official pages disagree on the CPS selection day: limited-enrolment page "Pharmacy: 6 February 2027" (Dutch Farmacie, shared 300-place table); CPS's selection page "Monday 1 March 2027 - Selection day for prospective students abroad", "Tuesday 2 March or Thursday 4 March 2027" for NL residents. Used the CPS page. CPS itself: "Each year a maximum of 60 students is admitted."
- IB subjects (IB terms, from the programme pages): CPS "Chemistry: Higher Level", "Physics: Standard Level", Maths AA SL or AI HL; EBE and PPE "Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches at at least Standard Level or Mathematics: Applications and Interpretation at Higher Level"; GSS the same maths plus 2 of Biology HL, Chemistry HL, Physics SL, Economics SL or Geography SL; MBLS (PDF IB row) "Mathematics: Analysis & Approaches at Higher Level, Biology at Higher Level, Chemistry at Higher Level and Physics at Standard Level". Humanities programmes list no IB subject requirement.
- English: EMI-ready, -experienced and -advanced pages all exempt an IB Diploma "with English A or B as final exam subject at either Standard or Higher Level". PPE needs EMI-advanced, others EMI-ready.
- Nothing contradicts data/countries/nl.json (admissionsUrl https://www.uu.nl/en/bachelors is a hub; ibPageUrl is the diploma-equivalency page used here).
- Regular deadlines: every UU programme page prints them without a year ("the latest date by which we can receive and process your application is 1 May"; non-EU "1 April"), so the dated entries (2027-05-01 eu-eea-ch, 2027-04-01 non-eu) cite Study in NL, which prints "1 May '27" / "1 Apr '27" for the 1 Sep '27 start (ELC page). The Nuffic feed gives the same 1 May/1 April for PPE and CPS too, which is wrong for those numerus fixus programmes; their own pages were used.
- Unverified / left out: Final-diploma deadline "31 August" is also yearless except on programme pages (CPS "31 August 2027", PPE "31 August, 23:59 CEST 2027"). BSA 45 EC: Humanities page "The BSA norm is 45 EC."; MBLS page says "For academic year 2025-2026, the norm is 45 EC." (no 2026-27 figure seen).
