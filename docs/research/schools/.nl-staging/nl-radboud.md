# nl-radboud (Radboud University), retrieved 2026-09-25

- Scope: listed, 14 English-taught bachelor's, all in Nijmegen (single campus, so no `city`).
- Hand-off: https://www.ru.nl/en/education/bachelors/overview/language/1378 (overview with the English language facet). It shows "Result 1 - 14 of 14 results", bachelor's only; the unfiltered overview shows 52.
- The landing page https://www.ru.nl/en/education/bachelors still says "13 Bachelor's programmes taught in English"; the filtered list has 14. I used the list, where every programme page gives English as its main language.
- National cross-check, excluded (Studiekeuze123 lists an English-taught variant, but Radboud's own page says Dutch): Dutch, European and International Law ("Voertaal: Nederlands (70%)"); Business Administration/Bedrijfskunde, Theology, Human Neuroscience, Geography, Planning and Environment, Biomedical Sciences, Political Science, Public Administration, Medicine and Philosophy ("The language of instruction of this programme is Dutch."); Mathematics ("Dutch (90%)"); Physics and Astronomy ("Dutch (80%)"). Filosofie's English variant is Philosophy, Politics and Society, which I included.
- National cross-check, excluded (Study in NL): German Language and Culture. Radboud's page says "The language of instruction of this programme is Dutch."
- Included but missing from the national sources: International Business Administration (in neither), and Philosophy, Politics and Society, Economics and Business Economics and Natural Sciences (on Studiekeuze123 only as variants or entries, not on Study in NL). All four are in Radboud's English facet, and each page gives English as its main language.
- American Studies ("Bachelor of Arts, specialisation of English Language and Culture") and English Language and Culture are separate entries: Radboud lists each with its own page and deadlines.
- Deadlines: every EU/EEA block for 2027 says "Applications open from 01 October 2026". Science programmes (AI, Biology, Chemistry, Computing Science, Natural Sciences) print "Application deadline 01 July 2027". The humanities, EBE and IBA blocks print "01 May 2027" but add "Apply by 1 May at the latest if you want to participate in the (SSH&) housing lottery. If you're not participating in the housing lottery, you can apply up to and including 1 July." The general deadlines page agrees: "1 May for EU/EEA students who want to participate in the SSH& housing lottery / 1 July for EU/EEA students who don't". So I recorded 1 May 2027 (housing lottery) and 1 July 2027 (final) as institution dates. Non-EU: "01 April 2027" on every programme page.
- Psychology is numerus fixus ("maximum capacity of 600 students"). Its block prints "Application deadline 15 January 2027", set as `closes`. The page also gives an OSIRIS file deadline (21 January), a matching assignment (15 February), ranking numbers (15 April) and an enrolment/diploma deadline (31 July), all without a year, so I left them out.
- Molecular Life Sciences and Philosophy, Politics and Society still show the 2026 cycle ("01 July 2026" and "01 May 2026"). I set no `closes`, so the institution-wide 2027 dates apply (the general deadlines page gives 1 July for EU/EEA). Study in NL gives MLS as EU 1 Jul / non-EU 1 Apr for 2027.
- IB rules: Biology, Chemistry, Computing Science, MLS and Natural Sciences have diploma-specific PDFs for "study year 2027-2028": 27 points excluding core, all grades 4+, plus subjects. The AI PDF is 2025-2026 (Maths AA HL 4+, 24 points, three HL), marked "2025/26 criteria; 2027 not yet published". The Psychology PDF has no year (uploaded 2025-09): Maths AI SL/HL 4+ or AA SL/HL 3+, 24 points, three HL.
- EBE, IBA, IBC, PPS and the humanities give no IB subject grades; they point to the Radboud Eligibility Check. The maths rule is paraphrased from the pages, and a maths test (SAT Maths 600+, OMPT, CCVX, Boswell) is the fallback.
- English: IB counts if English-taught, or English A SL/HL at 4+, or English B HL at 6+ (bachelor's English requirements page). The Biology page says simply "An International Baccalaureate Diploma (diploma programme)".
- Tuition: programme pages print "Academic year 2027 - 2028 / Statutory tuition fee €2,771.00" (Biology and others), so I used "€2,771 (2027/28 statutory fee)" per the coordinator. The EBE and IBA tuition pages still show "Academic year 2026 - 2027 €2,694.00"; I used the 2027/28 statutory figure for them too.
- Contradicts data/countries/nl.json: nothing material. nl.json has englishBachelors null, and admissionsUrl https://www.ru.nl/en/education/bachelors is a landing page; the English-filtered overview is the better hand-off.
- Unverified: IB subject grades for EBE, IBA, IBC, PPS and the humanities (the Eligibility Check is an interactive tool); the 2027 AI and Psychology criteria; any 2027 IB final-results deadline with a year printed (enrolment deadlines on the general page carry no year).
