# nl-uva: University of Amsterdam (retrieved 2026-09-25)

- Scope listed, 23 English-taught bachelor's (the UvA list's 24 English results minus AUC, which has its own record nl-auc). Taken from the list's JSON feed (`/_restapi/list-json`, field `programmeLanguage`) and each programme page's "Language of instruction English"; all 23 URLs fetched (HTTP 200, programme's own page).
- Hand-off: `bachelor-s-programmes.html?f=programmeLanguage%3Aenglish`, checked in a browser: bachelor's only, "Results: 24 of 64" (24 English incl. AUC; 64 bachelor's in total).
- Numerus fixus (English track only, deadline 15 January 2027): Business Administration (650 places, online test 17 or 27 Feb), Economics and Business Economics (850, test 20 Feb or 3 Mar), Political Science (345, unweighted lottery), Psychology (300, selection tests). PPLE is selective with a 1 February deadline (pple.uva.nl "Key dates for September 2027 intake"). Actuarial Science, BA, EBE, Political Science also have Dutch/bilingual tracks; only the English tracks are listed.
- IB rules from the UvA diploma finder, which says it "applies only to applications for the 2027–2028 academic year" (data in `/webfiles/.../diploma-finder/country_data.js`, key "International Baccalaureate"). Strictest: AA HL at grade 4 (Actuarial Science, Business Analytics, Econometrics and Data Science).
- PPLE's own per-diploma page still says "applies to the September 2026 intake"; the 34-point / Maths 4 rule is quoted from the 2027-2028 diploma finder instead, which agrees with it.
- Psychology: its own page says the diploma must include Mathematics at least at VWO Wiskunde A level ("mathematics has to be included in the upper secondary education curriculum up until the final year and final examinations"); the diploma finder only says AA SL/HL or AI HL at 4 is "highly recommended". Recorded both, without a grade floor.
- Dates: 1 April 2027 / 1 May 2027 are printed on the FEB and Social Sciences programme pages (e.g. Actuarial Science, Sociology). The Humanities programme application pages (Ancient Studies, Archaeology, English, European Studies, GACP, Linguistics, LCA, Media and Culture, Media and Information, Sign Language) still print "1 April 2026" / "1 May 2026"; the central page gives 1 April / 1 May with no year.
- Tuition: "€2,771 (2027/28 statutory fee)" per the coordinator, cited from UU Media and Culture ("statutory fee 2027-2028, full-time: € 2,771"); UvA's own fee page still shows only 2026-2027. PPLE charges EU students a higher statutory fee: "PPLE students €5,659" (2026-2027), recorded as such.
