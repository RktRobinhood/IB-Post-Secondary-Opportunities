# nl-rotterdam-uas (Rotterdam University of Applied Sciences / Hogeschool Rotterdam), retrieved 2026-09-25

- Scope: listed, 3 programmes: International Business (BBA, 4 years, 3-year Fast Track for VWO-level diplomas "i.e International Baccalaureate"), Fine Art (BA, 4 years) and Graphic Design (BA, 4 years). Fine Art and Graphic Design are at Willem de Kooning Academy (WdKA). All three are in Rotterdam, so no `city`.
- Hand-off: https://www.rotterdamuas.com/programmes/bachelor/. Checked in a browser, it shows "3 programmes" (Fine art, Graphic Design, International Business), bachelor's only. The list loads by JavaScript, so a plain fetch shows it empty.
- nl.json's claim ("now teaches only three bachelor's in English... International Business, and Fine Art and Graphic Design at Willem de Kooning Academy") matches rotterdamuas.com: each page says "Four-year bachelor in English". The Dutch site agrees: International Business "Geheel in het Engels", Vormgeving "Grafisch ontwerpen (ook Engels)", Autonome Beeldende Kunst "Taal: Nederlands en Engels".
- Nuance against nl.json: WdKA's own site has English pages and an "Admission in English, BA" route for all its Design majors (Advertising & Beyond, Animation, Audiovisual Design, Fashion, Illustration, Product Design, Spatial Design, Photography). But its FAQ says: "All our courses are officially taught in Dutch. However, we have international classrooms, which means that whenever there are international students present, the teachers will speak English." I kept the three the institution calls English-taught and put the other majors in a note. The same FAQ sentence also covers Fine Art and Graphic Design, so the three official pages and the WdKA FAQ disagree; I trusted the programme pages.
- National cross-check: Study in NL lists "Design" (BA, English), naming Advertising & Beyond, Animation, Audiovisual Design, Fashion Design, Graphic Design, Illustration, Product Design and Spatial Design. I included only Graphic Design, for the reason above, and set this out in note 1. Studiekeuze123's Vormgeving English variant maps to the same thing.
- National cross-check, excluded: Logistics Engineering (Studiekeuze123 English variant). The HR page says "Taal: Nederlands (jaar 1+2), Engels (jaar 3+4)", so first-years are taught in Dutch.
- Transformation Design (on WdKA's EN list) is excluded; its page says "no new students will be admitted to this programme". Dual Degree RASL (with Erasmus) is a 5-year double degree applied for at both institutions; I left it out as not a standalone bachelor of this institution.
- Dates: WdKA says "Applications for the 2027/2028 academic year are not open yet. You can apply from 1 October 2026." (recorded as opens 2026-10-01). WdKA's 2027 registration and entrance-exam round deadlines are not published (the page shows only 2026: "EU May 1, 2026 / NON-EU January 11, 2026"), so none are recorded.
- International Business deadlines: rotterdamuas.com prints "Non-EU: April 1st & EU/EEA: July 31st" without a year (the same page mentions the "September 2027 intake"). Study in NL prints "Start: 1 Sep '27, App. deadline EU/EEA 31 Jul '27, App. deadline Non-EU/EEA 1 Apr '27", which I used and cited.
- Study in NL also gives Fine Art and Design "1 May '27 (EU/EEA), 1 Mar '27 (Non-EU)". Not used: WdKA says its 2027 dates come later, and its 2026 non-EU deadline (11 January) conflicts with the pattern.
- IB rule: the diploma must equal HAVO/VWO/MBO 4. No subject requirements are stated. English exemption applies if "the medium of instruction of the previous education was in English"; the IB Diploma is not on the EU diploma exemption list (PDF). Otherwise IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL iBT 80 / Cambridge 169.
- Fast Track (3 years) needs VWO level (IB named) plus "Pass the intake interview with the Fast Track coordinator". Its page still carries stale "September 1st, 2023" wording for diploma declarations.
- Tuition: RUAS pages show "2026-2027 Statutory fee €2,694"; Study in NL shows 2027/2028 EU/EEA €2,771. I used "€2,771 (2027/28 statutory fee)" per the coordinator.
- Unverified: the 2027 WdKA entrance-exam round and registration deadlines; whether first-years in the other WdKA majors are taught in English in practice; any IB final-diploma deadline with the year printed ("31st of August" appears without a year).
