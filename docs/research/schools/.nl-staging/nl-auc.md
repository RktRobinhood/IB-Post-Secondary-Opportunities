# nl-auc: Amsterdam University College (retrieved 2026-09-25)

- Scope listed, 1 programme: Liberal Arts and Sciences, 3 years, a BSc for the Sciences major and a BA for Social Sciences or Humanities (programme-structure page). Joint UvA/VU degree.
- Hand-off: AUC "How to apply" page (single programme, so there is no list). It shows the one bachelor's and the September 2027 deadlines. No other levels.
- Dates for September 2027, all printed with the year on auc.nl: applications open 1 October 2026; early-bird 1 December 2026; regular 1 February 2027; decisions before 10 February / 10 April 2027; late 1 May 2027 only "If there is a late deadline", to be "announced in March 2027". No separate EU/non-EU dates.
- IB: AUC says "predicted grades (as in the International Baccalaureate system) are not accepted". The UvA diploma finder notes that the IB English exemption "may not apply to the PPLE and Liberal Arts and Sciences programmes". AUC lists "International Baccalaureate Standard level English at grade 5" as a possible alternative proof, otherwise IELTS 7.0 (6.5 each) or TOEFL 100.
- Maths per major (IB): Sciences AA HL 5 / AI HL 6 / AA SL 6 (with a STEM subject: AA HL 4 / AA SL 5 / AI HL 6); Social Sciences AI HL 6 / AA SL 5 / AA HL 4; Humanities AA SL 4 / AI SL 7 / AI HL 4.
- Tuition: AUC's fee page lists only 2026-2027 ("The statutory tuition fee for the AUC programme is €5580"). The 2027-28 figure isn't published, so the 2026/27 figure is recorded with its year.
- National cross-check: Studiekeuze123 "Liberal Arts and Sciences (joint degree)" (VU+UvA) and Study in NL "Liberal Arts and Sciences (Amsterdam University College)" with deadline 2027-02-01 for EU and non-EU both match. Nothing excluded.
- data/countries/nl.json: nothing contradicted. Its note "first-years live on campus" understates the page: "All students live in on-campus accomodation for the three years of their study" and "AUC guarantees accommodation for all enrolled students for three years".
- Unverified: English-proof deadline "by 1 July" (no year printed, so left out of dates).
