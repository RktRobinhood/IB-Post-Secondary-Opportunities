# School records, Norway (#43): admissions counsellor, round 1

Under review: the 14 records `data/schools/no-*.json`, and the Norwegian part of `docs/research/schools/reports/batch-se-no.md`. I checked them on 26 September 2026 against the institutions' own pages, Samordna opptak and Study in Norway (HK-dir). I used WebFetch, WebSearch and plain scripted fetches (curl) only. I did not use a browser.

## Score: 8/10. Accepted, on condition that E1 and E2 are fixed before commit.

**No deadline is wrong.** Eight records carry dates, 26 entries in all, and I checked every one against its source. All are right. The five `none` records (NTNU, UiB, UiT, NIH, OsloMet) record no dates, and that is also right. Samordna opptak's calendar still says "Gjeld for opptaket til universitet og høgskole i 2026", and each of those records says the 2027 calendar is unpublished rather than guessing.

The `none` scopes are right, and each states the way in, in IB terms and at the right grade (Samordna: Danish, Swedish or Norwegian A at SL or HL, "karakteren 3 eller bedre"). The Norwegian subject codes (R1, S1+S2, "1+2" sciences, MATTE4) are converted exactly by Samordna's IB table. The records also handle the hardest Norwegian question well: which route a student still at school can use. NMBU's and UiO's cards say plainly that the 1 December direct route needs the diploma in hand. NHH's and BI's cards say they take predicted grades. I would repeat almost all of it to a student.

Two points leave out, or misstate, what the official page tells a final-year IB student to do. Each is a one-line fix and needs no further round:

- **E1.** UiS Dance tells the student to ask UiS how to document an unfinished IB, when the page already says how.
- **E2.** NHH's Samordna route tells an IB student to upload the diploma by 1 July. Samordna's IB page says an IB school leaver cannot, and must use the IBO results service instead.

## Smaller points (fix when convenient, not blocking)

1. **no-ntnu `ib`** (and **no-nmbu `ib`**) list Danish, Norwegian or Swedish A, but leave out Norwegian B at HL. Samordna: "Norwegian A på Higher eller Standard level eller Norwegian B på Higher level dekker kravet i norsk" for diplomas from 2021. UiB, UiT, NIH and OsloMet include it. Make all six say the same.
2. **Dates copied from pages that give no year.** Nord's admission page ("1 February - 15 April", "1 July"), UiA's calendar, and the UiO, NMBU and UiS Dance pages give recurring dates with no year. Nord's own programme pages still say "15. April 2026 at 23:59". The records put these under 2027. That reading is reasonable: each is the next cycle, and INN and NHH do print 2027. But the brief says "don't guess from 2026". Either cite a page that prints 2027, or add "(page gives no year)" to the label, as the Finnish records did for unpublished criteria.
3. **no-khio `ib`** says "the IB Diploma meets the general entry requirement". The ballet page does not ask for general entry at all: "It is open to young dancers who have completed lower secondary school and are at least fifteen years old". Note 2 already says this, so the `ib` line and note 2 pull in different directions. Say "No school-leaving qualification needed: the audition decides."
4. **no-nmbu `dates[0]`**, the 1 December direct route, has `who: "eu-eea-ch"`. The route is for everyone without Nordic schooling or Norwegian, EU and non-EU alike ("The application period is 15 October - 1 December"). Use `any`.
5. **"Nordic schooling" and an IB taken in Denmark.** INN's page reads: "applicants with upper secondary education from the Nordic countries, and all applicants who meet the Norwegian requirement, are to apply on Samordna opptak". NMBU, UiA and NTNU draw similar lines. A student at an IB school in Denmark will want to know which side of that line they are on. NMBU's note is honest that NMBU does not say. INN's `apply` ("Samordna opptak (Nordic schooling)") should quote INN's words, so the student can see that "upper secondary education from the Nordic countries" plausibly includes their Danish IB.
6. **no-nhh `notes[0]`**: "NHH says you must apply through Samordna opptak". NHH's page says students with Norwegian proficiency "should refer to the Norwegian language version of the website and follow the application process through Samordna Opptak". "Should" is closer to the page.
7. **Reporting gap.** `progress.md` has no Norway section. `reports/batch-se-no.md` covers only four of the 14 Norwegian records (UiB, UiT, NIH, OsloMet). BI, INN, KHiO, NHH, NMBU, Nord, NTNU, UiA, UiO and UiS have no report, so nothing records their hand-off counts or their contradictions of `data/countries/no.json`. The brief requires a `progress.md` section.

## For the brief (carry to the next countries)

- **When a national portal has an IB-specific upload page, it overrides the generic document deadline.** Samordna's "you will not manage to upload by 1 July" is exactly the sort of line a school page leaves out (E2).
- **Before telling a student to "ask the university", check that the page doesn't already answer.** (E1)

## Summary

- Score: **8/10, accepted**, on condition that E1 and E2 are fixed before commit (one line each; no further round needed).
- Errors: **2** (E1 UiS, a material omission; E2 NHH, a document instruction an IB student cannot follow) and **0 wrong deadlines** (26 of 26 dates correct), plus 7 smaller points.
