# School records, Sweden (#43): admissions counsellor, round 1

Under review: the 14 records `data/schools/se-*.json`, and the Swedish part of `docs/research/schools/reports/batch-se-no.md`. I checked them on 26 September 2026 against the institutions' own pages, universityadmissions.se and antagning.se. I used WebFetch, WebSearch and plain scripted fetches (curl) only. I did not use a browser.

## Score: 6/10. Not accepted.

**No deadline is wrong.** I checked every date in all 14 records: 97 date entries, against University Admissions' autumn-2027 calendar and the institutions' own pages. Every one is right. The antagning.se IB conversions are right too, and so is every maths conversion I traced. This is careful work.

The round fails on a different question, the one that matters most to a student in their final IB year: **which round can I actually use?** University Admissions' IB page answers it without hedging:

> "If you haven't yet completed your IB Diploma programme, **do not apply to the first admissions round**."

A May-2027 IB candidate can only use the second (national) round, 15 March to 15 April 2027, and only as an EU/EEA citizen. KI, KTH, GU, LU and SU say so on their cards. Six records do not:

- **LTU and UmU tell the student to ask whether a January application could work.** LTU's own programme pages say the opposite.
- **LiU lists only the January round** and never says the student cannot use it.
- **Malmö and SLU recommend the January round**, repeating the university's advice to international students, with no IB caveat.
- **Uppsala promises every programme a national round.** Its own pages show none for these programmes.

Between them these cover 23 of the 59 programmes. The root cause is in the researcher's report: it quotes the second half of University Admissions' IB guidance ("apply to the second admissions round only if you're a citizen of an EU/EEA country") but not the first sentence, "do not apply to the first admissions round".

The fix is mechanical: one rule, applied the same way on every Swedish card. It needs one more round of review, because the rule changes what six cards tell a student to do.

**The fix for E1–E6, as one rule on every Swedish card:**
- Label the first round "January round: only if you already hold your IB Diploma".
- Where a programme has a second-round occasion, label it "April round: the round for May 2027 IB candidates (EU/EEA)".
- Where it has none yet, say "No April round published for 2027: as a May 2027 IB candidate you cannot apply yet."
- Keep a programme's own exception (UmU Industrial Design) on that programme.

## Smaller points (fix when convenient, not blocking)

It leaves out **Fine Arts**, the tenth programme on Lund's English list ("Lund University offers ten international Bachelor's programmes"). Add it to the note, and give the reason: the Malmö Art Academy's 2027 cycle is not yet published.
6. **se-umu `dates[0]`**, "opens, 13:00 CET": I could not find the time on UmU's key-dates page or on University Admissions' page. Cite the page that gives it, or drop the time.
7. **"Final IB results due … 5 July" is recorded with `who: any`** in GU, KTH, LU, SU, UU, Chalmers, SLU and UmU. It is a second-round (EU/EEA) deadline ("IB/EU upper secondary results deadline: 5 July"). Set `who: "eu-eea-ch"` (KI and MAU already do).

## For the brief (carry to Denmark-adjacent countries)

- **Quote a portal's rule for final-year students in full, and apply it on every card.** Sweden's is one sentence ("do not apply to the first admissions round"). Where a programme has no round a final-year student can use, the card must say so in words. It must not suggest asking the university.
- **An institution's general advice ("apply in the first round") is written for diploma holders.** Don't pass it on to an IB candidate without the IB caveat.

## Summary

- Score: **6/10, not accepted.**
- Errors: **6 material round errors** (E1 LTU, E2 UmU, E3 LiU, E4 MAU, E5 SLU, E6 UU) and **0 wrong deadlines** (97 of 97 dates correct), plus 8 smaller points.
- Most important fix: **state the rule on every Swedish card**: a final-year IB student may not use the January round, and where no April 2027 round is published (LTU, LiU, UmU Life Science and IBE, very likely Uppsala), say so.
