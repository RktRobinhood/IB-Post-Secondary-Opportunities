Written before the first critic round, using what rounds 1–3 of `docs/research/qa/country-audit-europe/` rejected.

Principle: student-facing text is advice, not a research log. Every date, fee and rule is kept. Removed or rewritten: narration of how the site was edited or how a page was fetched; unsupported ranking claims ("the best", "the most", "the only", "world-class", "Asia's most…") where no official source in the record supports them; and internal contradictions — calendar entries marked a hard deadline whose own note says there is no deadline or that late applications are still considered, and short versions or watch-outs that disagreed with the calendar.
