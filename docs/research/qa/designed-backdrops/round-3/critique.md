## Blocking: the window assumes three columns; the page has two

The dealer and guard look back 3 cards, "covering above and below in a 3-column grid".
But school pages at 1280 px are **2 columns** (the deadlines sidebar takes the third).
The card directly below-but-one in the same column is 4 away, so look-alikes land two
rows apart in the same column, where the eye catches them straight away:

- **PW engineering:**
  - **Civil Engineering and Mechatronics are near twins:** diagonal hatch at the same
    angle, with a violet disc at the top right. They are in the same column, two rows
    apart.
  - Aerospace and Environmental Engineering are both diagonal grid, also in the same
    column, two rows apart.
- **RUG humanities:**
  - Arts, Culture and Media and Religious Studies are both diagonal hatch in sand
    (this is the known limit).
  - American Studies and Media Studies are both sand arcs.

In both sections there are fewer designed cards (6 and 7) than look families (8). So a
look-alike-free deal is always possible.

**Fix:** within one field section, do not repeat a look family until every family has
been used. Where a section is longer than that, use a look-back of at least 5 (two rows
of a 2-column grid). Make the guard use the same rule. That is all that stands between
this and 8.
