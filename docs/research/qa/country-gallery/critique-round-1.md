# Country gallery critique, round 1

Reviewed: contact sheets 1-3 (29 countries, 145 new tiles; Denmark column 1 included), against docs/research/country-gallery/picks.jsonl.
Judged from contact-sheet resolution only (tiles are about 16:10 already). Anything I could read on the sheet counts as legible at card size.

## Score: 7 / 10

The set is strong on the brief. Most countries read as five different experiences (city, nature, culture, student/everyday life, season) and are bright and clean. It is not shippable today because 17 tiles break a hard rule, and they are concentrated in a few countries: night or dim street scenes, legible signage, a crowd, a white-out, and one aerial. HK, CN, PT, LV and AT lose several slides each.

## Score with the 17 rejects removed: 8.5 / 10

Rejects are replaced by a fresh pick, not left as holes; the 8.5 assumes the cards still cycle 5 slides. The remaining tiles are true, pleasant, varied photos.
