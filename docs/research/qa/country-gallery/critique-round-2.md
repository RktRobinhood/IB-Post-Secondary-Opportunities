## Score: 7.5 / 10

Clear gain on round 1: no night-murk, no crowds, no text-dominated tiles except one. Leads are mostly strong (Moraine Lake, Kinderdijk, Central Park, Sydney ferry are card-ready). Held back by a lead that breaks the text/logo rule (AE), one drab hazy panorama (AU 6.5) and one drab dim canal (LV 26.4).

## Score with the 3 rejects removed: 8.5 / 10

Assumes each reject is replaced by a bright daylight pick so cards still cycle five slides. AE then has no lead and only three slides (Hatta, Al Qudra, Corniche), so it needs a new lead first.
