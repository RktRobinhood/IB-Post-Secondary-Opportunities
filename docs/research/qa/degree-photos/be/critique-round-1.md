# Belgium degree photos: photo editor, round 1, 6/10 (30 Sep 2026)

I judged `contact-sheet-round-1.jpg` (18 photos). I checked 12 of them at 720 px in
`src/assets/img/programmes/school-be-*-720.webp`, including the four the researcher
flagged, and read every line in `docs/research/programme-images/schools-be.jsonl`. I
compared the set with the accepted German set (`../de/contact-sheet-round-2.jpg`) and
the accepted Swedish set (`../se/contact-sheet-round-2.jpg`).

## Score: 6/10

The core is good and varied: a nursing student drawing up a dose, gloved hands on an
engine, a laser cutter, a VR tester, probes on a chip, the KU Leuven reading room, river
sampling and an outdoor podcast. There are only three lab-type benches and no text
problems. But five cards fail, and three of the five are about meaning, not polish:

- one card shows the wrong degree at its own school;
- one is a US court with the American flag for a European law degree;
- one pair of degrees at Howest look alike and also repeat a Swedish subject.

**With the 5 rejects below removed, the remaining 13 score 8/10.**

## Flagged but kept

- **17** `school-be-thomas-more-international-tourism-and-leisure` (the cave tour): **keep
  with reservation.** It is a small group of adults behind a guide with a headlamp. Faces
  are small, and it is not an event crowd. It is exactly the "guide with a small group,
  faces small" direction I gave Germany's tourism replacement in round 1. The warm cave
  light is attractive. The weakness is that the guide does not stand out from the group;
  a frame where the guide is clearly leading would be better.

## Directions for replacements (candidates, not prescriptions)

- **1 Digital Arts & Entertainment:** a well-lit motion-capture volume with a performer
  mid-move and no studio wordmark, or hands sculpting a character on a drawing tablet or
  in clay maquette.
- **9 DEVINE:** not paper and not a screen close-up. Options: device testing (hands on
  several phones and tablets on a test rig), or a user-testing session seen from behind
  a one-way mirror, faces small.
- **13 International Journalism:** see the Thomas More note above. Nothing with cars.
- **15 International & European Law:** a European courtroom such as the Court of Justice
  of the EU in Luxembourg or a Belgian courthouse, empty or with small robed figures,
  and no national flags at the edges. Keep it distinct from whatever Sweden's Malmö
  human-rights replacement uses; I suggested the European Court of Human Rights there,
  so prefer the CJEU or a Belgian court here.
- **18 Urban Sustainability:** people doing it in a city, preferably Belgian or Dutch.
  Options: depaving a street, planting trees in a square, or a researcher measuring on
  a city green roof with rooftops behind.
