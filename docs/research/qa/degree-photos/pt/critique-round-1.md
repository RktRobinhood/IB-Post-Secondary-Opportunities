the catalogue:

- 3, cortical neurons glowing teal;
- 5, a spray operator in shield and mask against a mud-brick wall.

The sixth, the first official photo, is a real ISEG class. But it shows teaching, not
management: it could head any degree at any university.

**With the 1 reject below removed, the remaining 5 score 8/10.**

### The official photo's source, confirmed

- `sourcePage` is `https://www.iseg.ulisboa.pt/en/2022/11/tutors-and-graders-applications-open-until-10-march-2023/`.
  That is ISEG's own site, and ISEG is the University of Lisbon's School of Economics
  and Management. The page returns 200. Its `og:site_name` is "ISEG" and its `og:image`
  is exactly the `officialUrl`.
- `officialUrl` is `https://www.iseg.ulisboa.pt/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Graders.jpg`,
  on ISEG's own WordPress uploads. It returns 200, as an image/jpeg of about 170 kB.
- Caveats for the coordinator:
  - The page is a 2022 notice recruiting tutors and graders, not a programme page.
  - At full size the projected slide is a human-resources training slide, as the crop
    note says.
  - The line carries no licence or author field, unlike every Commons line. If the site
    credits official images, that field is missing.

## Flagged but kept

- **4** `school-pt-ist-general-engineering-geni` (the Pelamis hinged joints): **yes, it
  now reads differently enough from Italy's MOSE (IT #19).**
  - MOSE is an upright red lattice platform standing in a lagoon, with shore and horizon
    behind.
  - This is a tight diagonal of pink-red tubes lying in the swell, with the black ram
    shafts of two joints at centre and no horizon.
  - At card size one reads as "a structure in the water" and the other as "a mechanism".

  Keep with reservation: there are no people, and both cards are still red-in-water.
  Neither country should add another.
- **1** `school-pt-uac-ocean-sciences` (the "marum 1705" plate): **keep with
  reservation.** At 720 px the plate is just legible. At 480 px it is a few pixels
  high, sits on the upper half of the ROV away from the title, and is the research
  institute's name, not a consumer brand. The photograph is one of the best in the set.
- **5** `school-pt-nova-global-public-health` (the agency stripe on the hard hat):
  **keep.** The blue band's lettering is illegible even at 720 px, and it sits at the
  top edge, well clear of the title. It is a strong, dignified frame of prevention being
  done, with one adult and no children.
