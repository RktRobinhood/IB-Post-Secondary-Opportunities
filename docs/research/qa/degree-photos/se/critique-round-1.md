# Sweden degree photos: photo editor, round 1, 5/10 (30 Sep 2026)

I judged `contact-sheet-round-1.jpg` (40 photos). I checked 30 of them at 720 px in
`src/assets/img/programmes/school-se-*-720.webp` and read every line in
`docs/research/programme-images/schools-se.jsonl`. I also compared the set with the
accepted German set (`../de/contact-sheet-round-2.jpg`) to catch repeated subjects.

## Score: 5/10

The craft, music and field cards are some of the best in the catalogue: the forge, the
hands on walnut, the organist, the quartet, the Abisko flux chamber, the MAX IV hutch.
But 18 of the 40 break a hard rule, and they cluster:

- **Games.** Five of the six game degrees are game-jam photos of people at screens
  (25–30). Four of them come from one event (Global Game Jam 2012) and sit side by side
  on Uppsala's page.
- **Politics at Malmö.** The cards are assembly halls, a vote board and a
  soldier-and-civilian press photo (34–38).
- **Text and logos.** A chalkboard proof, a UN tally board, a scanned sketch sheet, a
  Nescafé jar, an Eppendorf robot, a Coke can, a supermarket's branding, and a studio
  wordmark.

A set with this many failures cannot be accepted. **With the 18 rejects below removed,
the remaining 22 score 8/10.** Every one of them shows the discipline or its defining
instrument, with no legible text, logo, event or child.

**Strong (keep as they are):** 1 forge, 2 hands on walnut, 5 string quartet, 7 opera
rehearsal, 8 organist, 11 lathe boring (close-up), 12 warehouse, 14 pipetting in the
hood, 18 MAX IV NanoMAX hutch, 20 flux chamber on the Abisko mire, 22 girth tape on
bark, 24 excavation in a rock chamber.

## Keep with reservations

These are the first to replace when a better photo turns up.

- **3** `school-se-gu-bfa-programme-in-craft-with-specialisation-in-crafting-futures`: an empty bench with no hands, and it is the second wood card at HDK-Valand after 2. A non-wood craft (ceramics, textile, glass) would separate it from 2.
- **10** `school-se-ju-prosthetics-and-orthotics-bsc`: the thermoplastic draping is exactly right, but the US Air Force camouflage and unit patch fill the left third, so it reads as a military hospital.
- **13** `school-se-ltu-bachelor-programme-in-mineral-resource-engineering`: flotation froth is pure texture. It is beautiful and distinctive, but at card size a student sees grey bubbles, not mining.
- **17** `school-se-umu-bachelor-of-science-programme-in-life-science`: a sequencer cartridge close-up, abstract at 480 px. It is acceptable only because it is not another bench.
- **21** `school-se-su-bachelor-s-programme-in-earth-science-distance-learning`: the geologist is tiny, so it reads as a sagebrush landscape. It is also close in mood to 20 (a figure in open country with mountains behind).
- **26** `school-se-uu-bachelor-s-programme-in-game-design-and-graphics`: a person at a monitor, but the dragon artwork on screen makes the discipline readable. It must be the only screen on Uppsala's page.
- **32** `school-se-mau-interaction-design`: wireframe sketches with scrawled German words ("Begriff", "keyword") in the upper right. It is borderline at 480 px and saved because they are labels on a sketch, not a sentence.
- **34** `school-se-mau-european-studies-politics-societies-and-cultures-bachelor-s-programme`: the European Parliament hemicycle is a crowd shot, but it is iconic and the faces are tiny. It is one of only two assembly halls allowed in the country.
- **36** `school-se-gu-bachelor-s-program-in-international-relations`: the Security Council in session is event photography, saved by the Krohg mural and the horseshoe table. It is the second and last assembly hall.
- **39** `school-se-uu-bachelor-s-programme-in-holocaust-and-genocide-studies`: the wall of Arolsen index drawers is strong, but blank black exhibit boards and a touchscreen table clutter the foreground. A tighter crop on the drawers would lift it.

## Notes for the replacement batch

1. **Text again.** Four rejects (19, 33, 35, 40) are about legible words, and three
   more (15, 16, 31) involve a brand name. Check every candidate at 480 px, and read the
   crop note against the actual crop: on 15 and 31 the notes said the label fell under
   the veil, and it does not.
2. **Uppsala games needs four different looks,** with 26 (the on-screen drawing) the
   only screen. Candidates, not prescriptions:
   - level design: a physical grey-box or tabletop level model under construction, or a
     playtester's hands on a gamepad with the screen out of focus;
   - project management: a team around a table moving paper cards or tokens (and then
     not 25's look);
   - programming: hands soldering or wiring a controller or dev board, or a VR headset
     playtest.
   None of them may repeat Game Jam 2012 frames.
3. **LTU games (25, 30, 31):** re-crop 25 without the can. Look again for a mocap stage
   without the wordmark for 31. For 30, choose a subject no other games card uses, such
   as the inside of an arcade cabinet, a console dev kit, or motion-controller testing.
   It must not be a room of PCs.
4. **Malmö politics:** 34 and 36 are the country's only assembly halls. Replace 37, 35
   and 38 with things a camera can see being done and no chamber. Directions:
   - human rights: a courtroom such as the European Court of Human Rights, empty or
     with small figures, or a field investigator's hands;
   - international relations: a negotiation table set with flags, no crowd and no
     emblem;
   - peace and conflict: demining in progress, or a mediation circle seen from behind.
     No weapons at centre and no identifiable civilians.
5. **Look for Sweden.** Only 18 (MAX IV), 20 (Abisko) and 9 (Umeå, rejected) are Swedish
   places. Where it costs nothing, prefer a Swedish subject:
   - energy transition: a Swedish wind farm or hydropower station, or a heat-pump
     installer;
   - architecture: model-making or a studio crit at Umeå;
   - folk music: a close-up of nyckelharpa hands;
   - retail: a stockroom, shelf-planning or merchandising hands with no brand
     packaging legible.
6. **Cross-country repeats.** 23 is rejected for repeating Germany's solar crew. 11
   (lathe) and 12 (warehouse) touch German subjects (DE #14 lathe, DE #15 container
   loading) but look quite different, so they stay. No replacement may be another
   lathe, solar array, pipette-in-hood or code screen.
7. **Lab density is fine** once 15 and 16 go: 14 and 17 remain, plus 10's fabrication
   lab. Put no further wet-lab benches among the replacements.
8. **Light and dark:** 6, 8 and 26 are already dark. 8 survives on its warm wood. The
   white-dominant frames (19, 33) were the worst under a white title, and both are
   rejected.
