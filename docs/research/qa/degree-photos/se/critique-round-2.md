# Sweden degree photos: photo editor, round 2, 8/10 (30 Sep 2026)

I judged `contact-sheet-round-2.jpg`: the 22 photos left after the coordinator removed
all 18 round-1 rejects, renumbered. The 18 degrees that lost their photo fall back to
their school's photo until a replacement batch, which gets its own critique.

## Score: 8/10, accepted as it would ship

Every card now shows the discipline being done or its defining instrument. No card has
text that fights the title, and none has a logo, event crowd, child, map, or generic
person at a monitor. The strongest are worth stopping for: 1 forge, 2 hands on walnut,
4 string quartet, 6 organist, 8 lathe boring, 13 MAX IV hutch, 14 the Abisko flux
chamber, 16 girth tape on bark, and 17 excavation in a rock chamber.

It is not higher than 8 because six of the 22 still carry a reservation. The set also
says little about Sweden: only 13 and 14 are Swedish places.
