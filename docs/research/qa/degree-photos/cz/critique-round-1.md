# Czechia degree photos: photo editor, round 1, 3/10 (30 Sep 2026)

I judged `contact-sheet-round-1.jpg` (29 photos) and read all 29 lines in
`docs/research/programme-images/schools-cz.jsonl`. I opened the doubtful crops at
720 px (`src/assets/img/programmes/school-cz-*-720.webp`). I searched every other
`schools-*.jsonl` for each subject, and checked against the five Polish photos just
accepted: the PUMS phantom head, the UJ loupes, the MUG simulation ward, the AMU NMR
tubes and the UWr stained slides.

"Rescue by crop" means the stored 16:10 crop can be fixed by moving its
object-position. None of the rejects below can be: each problem is the subject
itself, not its framing.

## Score: 3/10

Eighteen of 29 cards fail:

- **Seven repeat a subject another country already uses:**
  - the blacksmith (SE metal art);
  - protein crystals (NL Radboud);
  - the physio leg massage, the third such card (ES CEU, NL Hanze);
  - solar installers (DE HSRW);
  - an acting rehearsal (NO Nord);
  - a painting studio (DE BCB);
  - a kneeling-in-a-crop-field researcher (the agriculture field card, DE Leuphana).
- **Three put a face first:** the endoscopy patient, the dental patient and the sepia
  coach.
- **Two are trade, not degree:** the lineman and the solar installer.
- **Five show nothing being done:**
  - a turbine runner on display;
  - a rehab gym of boxed bikes;
  - two empty lab rooms (nanotechnology, pharmacy);
  - military technicians unboxing a scanner.
- **The pumpjack is soft and cluttered.**
- **The tokamak is a jumble with no focal point.**

Several of these break more than one rule.

The core is good. Six cards are as strong as anything in the catalogue:

- 2, looking down into the reactor pool;
- 3, core logging;
- 5, the molten-iron pour;
- 7, the combine in its dust;
- 10, the zebrafish;
- 11, the rivets under the cutting flame.

## Directions for replacements (candidates, not prescriptions)

**Engineering and physical sciences**

- **CTU physical engineering:** a plasma glowing in a discharge tube or a
  tokamak-interior plasma frame.
- **CTU mechanical engineering:** hands on a lathe or milling setup is taken (DK), so
  try a wind-tunnel smoke line over a model, or an engine on a test bed.
- **CTU biomedical technology:** a person being fitted with an EMG armband, or hands
  assembling a prosthetic socket. Not a 3D-printed hand (ES UC3M).
- **VSB electrical engineering and BUT electrical engineering** need two different
  subjects:
  - a high-voltage lab arc;
  - a power-electronics board under a thermal camera;
  - hands at a motor test bench.
  - Not a substation (ES), an oscilloscope (DE) or solar (DE).
- **BUT mechanical fundamentals:** a CNC mill cutting with coolant, or a gear being
  measured with a micrometer. Not a forge, not TIG welding (IT).
- **UPOL petroleum:** a drill floor crew making up pipe, or a drill bit coming up.
  Anything sharp.
- **VSB nanotechnology:** a single wafer or thin film showing interference colours,
  held in tweezers. It is a subject, not a room.

**Life sciences, medicine and health**

- **USB biological chemistry:** hands loading an SDS-PAGE gel, or a column
  chromatography band. Not crystals, and not the AT gel under UV.
- **MUNI pharmacy:** hands filling capsules or pressing tablets in a teaching
  pharmacy. Not a mortar (IT), a tablet test (NL) or an empty lab.
- **MUNI general medicine:** a defibrillation drill on a manikin torso, or a
  stethoscope on a back with no face. Not ultrasound (IT UniTo), laparoscopy (IT
  Cattolica) or loupes (PL UJ).
- **CU dentistry:** hands at an intra-oral scanner, or a stone cast being trimmed.
  Not a chair, a phantom head (PL) or a wax crown (IT).
- **CU and UPOL physiotherapy** need two different subjects, neither a leg on a
  couch:
  - gait training between parallel bars, seen from behind;
  - kinesiology taping on a shoulder;
  - hydrotherapy in a pool.
- **CU coach:** a coach's hands spotting a gymnast, in colour and seen from the
  side. Or a coach with a stopwatch at a track, face small.

**Agriculture and the arts**

- **CZU agriculture and food:** food rather than field. Options: cheese curd being
  cut, or grain being tested in a hand. Not a kneeling researcher.
- **AMU authorial acting:** a mask or movement class shot wide, or a puppet being
  worked (a Czech strength). Not a two-person rehearsal.
- **AAU visual art studies:** printmaking, with a roller inking a block, or an art
  historian in raking light is taken (NL). A textless etching press in use is good.
