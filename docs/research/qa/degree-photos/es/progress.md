# Spanish degree photos (issue #54, third country batch)

One Wikimedia Commons photograph per degree in `data/schools/es-*.json` (scope
`listed`), keyed `school-<key>-<slug>` in `data/programme-images.json`. Method as
the Finland pilot and the France batch: subject, not campus (#51); free licence
only (CC0, PD, CC BY, CC BY-SA); no photograph reused anywhere in the manifest or
in `data/images.json`; every stored 960 px crop viewed before signing.

Scope: 93 programme paths across 11 listed schools (UAM and Navarra are
`scope: none`). IE University first (28 paths), then the rest.

Workflow, so a stopped run can resume:

1. Candidates are searched on Commons and viewed as numbered contact sheets
   (temporary, under `D:\ibp-tmp\es\sheets`, not kept).
2. Picks go into `docs/research/programme-images/schools-es.jsonl` (the research
   file the importer reads) and are fetched with
   `node scripts/import-programme-images.mjs --unsigned`.
3. Each stored crop in `src/assets/img/programmes/school-es-*.webp` is viewed.
   Rejects are replaced (delete the stored file, change the line, re-run).
4. Only after the whole batch is viewed is the importer run without
   `--unsigned`; a card whose photograph was not good enough is left out of the
   research file entirely, so it keeps its school's photograph.
