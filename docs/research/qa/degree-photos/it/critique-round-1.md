# Italy degree photos: photo editor, round 1, 7/10 (30 Sep 2026)

I judged `contact-sheet-round-1.jpg` (38 photos). I opened 18 stored files at 720 px
(`src/assets/img/programmes/school-it-*-720.webp`): every flagged card and every card
whose crop note mentions text or a badge. I read all 38 lines in
`docs/research/programme-images/schools-it.jsonl`. I compared the set with every
accepted set: DE, SE, BE, AT, NO, CH and NL.

## Score: 7/10

This is the most Italian-feeling set we have had.

**Italian and institution-tied frames:**

- 2, Parmigiano curd lifted from the copper vat;
- 12, Padua's own anatomy theatre;
- 19, MOSE in the Venice lagoon;
- 28, the Cave of the Sibyl;
- 34, the Virgo arm.

**Medicine is handled well.** Seven Medicine & Surgery degrees get seven different
clinical acts: anatomy theatre, otoscope, pathology microscope, surgical team,
ultrasound, stethoscope and blood draw. Across the country that is exactly right.

**The best photographs:**

- 3, a wax crown carved on a dental model;
- 5, lava scooped from a flow;
- 9, a TIG arc;
- 10, green streamlines over a car in a wind tunnel;
- 13, an otoscope;
- 26, a rainbow wafer;
- 33, sandplay;
- 36, a tunnel-boring machine breaking through.

But seven cards fail:

- a legible name badge;
- two repeats of patterns other countries were refused for (dishes in a landscape, and
  head gear in the psychology lab);
- a look-alike of 28, a corridor receding to a lit arch;
- three frames with nothing being done that do not earn it: a building facade, calves
  behind bars, and murky water.

The set also leans on equipment and empty places: 13 of the 38 have no hands in them.
That is acceptable for a striking instrument, but it is the ceiling.

**With the 7 rejects below removed, the remaining 31 score 8/10.**

## Flagged but kept

- **9** `school-it-polito-mechanical-engineering-entirely-in-english` (TIG welding):
  **keep. It is strong.** A human welder's hands and an arc read completely differently
  from NL #57's robot cell. This is the better welding picture of the two.
- **20** `school-it-unibo-economics-of-tourism-and-cities` (Rimini umbrellas): **keep
  with reservation.** No one is there, but the furled umbrellas under a night-blue sky
  are the most arresting tourism frame in the catalogue, and unmistakably the Riviera
  economy. If Spain's accepted beach card is also rows of umbrellas, one of them must
  change. From the brief, I understand Spain's is a beach, not this kind of night
  still-life.
- **38** `school-it-sapienza-sustainable-building-engineering` (Helsinki timber frame):
  **keep with reservation.** It is empty, but glulam columns and CLT slabs are exactly
  the material the degree is about, and nothing else in the catalogue shows them. The
  wrapped packs along the bottom carry a red printed pattern that is illegible and sits
  under the veil.

## MOSE (19) and Portugal

**I am keeping 19** (`school-it-polito-civil-and-environmental-engineering`, the red
MOSE jack-up platform in the lagoon). It is large, sharp and uniquely Venetian, and the
painted lettering on the deck is illegible at 480 px. **Tell Portugal:** a red machine
standing in open water is now taken, so the Pelamis wave machine must go or be a
clearly different frame (for example, a close-up of the hinge joints with a technician,
not a red hull in the sea).

## Directions for replacements (candidates, not prescriptions)

- **7 Criminology:** not a corridor or cell. Options: forensic hands dusting for prints
  or bagging evidence (no readable labels), or a courtroom dock seen from behind.
- **18 Electronic & Communications Engineering:** a technician climbing a telecom mast,
  or hands tuning an RF board with a probe. Not an anechoic chamber (DE #20).
- **22 Psychological Science:** nothing worn on the head. Options: eye-tracking from
  behind, a reaction-time box under a hand, or a one-way-mirror observation room.
- **23 Animal Care:** a carer's hands at work with a non-bovine animal: grooming a
  horse, checking a sheep's hooves, or handling poultry. No bars in the foreground.
- **27 Ancient Civilizations:** a clear, sunlit dive over a Roman mosaic with a diver
  recording, or hands cleaning a mosaic on land. Not murky water.
- **32 Global Law:** people doing law, such as a moot court from behind or a lawyer's
  hands on case files. No facades, no flags and no US courts.
- **35 Medicine & Surgery (Unibo):** any stethoscope or clinical frame with no name
  badge or patch. It must not repeat the other six medical acts.
