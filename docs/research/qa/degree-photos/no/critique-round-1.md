# Norway degree photos: photo editor, round 1, 6/10 (30 Sep 2026)

I judged `contact-sheet-round-1.jpg` (11 photos). I opened the stored files
`src/assets/img/programmes/school-no-*.webp` for 5, 6, 7, 8 and the four landscape
cards, and read every line in `docs/research/programme-images/schools-no.jsonl`. I
compared the set with the accepted German, Swedish, Belgian and Austrian sets.

## Score: 6/10

The performing-arts core is lovely and no other country has it:

- 1, dancers at the barre;
- 2, a contemporary partnering rehearsal;
- 3, patch cables on a modular synth;
- 10, a timpanist with mallets over copper drums.

9, a tea picker at work on a green slope, is warm and dignified.

But three of the 11 fail, and one of those is a defect the research line says was
fixed and was not.

**With the 3 rejects below removed, the remaining 8 score 8/10.**

## Flagged but kept

- **8** `school-no-inn-bachelor-in-game-art-and-animation` (the 1956 creature maquette):
  **keep.** Judged on its own, it is one of the best frames in the set: sharp,
  dramatic, and hands with a sculpting tool on a creature model. That is exactly what
  game art's character and creature design is. The black and white suits it under the
  veil. It is Norway's only archive photo; keep it that way.

## Keep with reservations

These are the first to replace when a better photo turns up. None blocks shipping.

- **4** `school-no-nord-bachelor-in-acting-and-artistic-production`: a flat, evenly lit school stage with an ironing board. The script's green cover carries a title that is legible at 720 px, though not at 480. It is honest rehearsal, but no one would stop scrolling for it.
- **7** `school-no-nord-bachelor-in-cg-art-and-animation`: a stop-motion miniature street is a good, unusual subject. But the degree is CG, so a physical set slightly mis-sells it. The left third is backstage clutter and a blue tarp, and the animator is small at the far end. With 8 also physical, Norway's two animation degrees both show models. They are different schools and look different, so both stay.

**Strong (keep as they are):** 1 ballet barre, 2 dance rehearsal, 3 modular synth, 8
creature maquette, 9 tea harvest, 10 timpanist.

## School pages checked for look-alikes

- **Nord (4, 5, 6, 7):** stage, shore, calf and model street. Distinct. 5 and 6 are
  rejected for defects, not look-alikes.
- **UiA (3, 10):** synth and timpani. Two music cards, distinct.
- **KHiO (1), UiS (2), INN (8), NMBU (9), UiO (11):** single cards. Ballet (1) and
  dance (2) are at different schools and look different: a barre line versus
  partnering.

## Cross-country repeats

None among the kept cards. Ballet, contemporary dance, modular synth, timpani, stage
acting, stop-motion, creature sculpting and tea harvest are all new to the catalogue.
Sweden has the other music cards (quartet, organ, opera), which are different
instruments and settings.

## Directions for replacements (candidates, not prescriptions)

- **5 Biology:** re-crop the same file; see the table.
- **6 Animal Science:** a vet or technician examining a cow, sheep or reindeer, with no
  children in frame. A Norwegian farm or reindeer herding with hands on the animal
  would be ideal.
- **11 Technology Systems:** people doing space or systems work. Not a CubeSat cleanroom
  (Germany has one) and not an empty dish. A sounding rocket being prepared at Andøya
  Space would be Norwegian and ideal, if Commons has a textless frame. Other options:
  hands wiring a drone or a satellite ground-station receiver.

## Light and dark

8 and 10 are dark and hold up on their subject. 2 and 11 are bright and flat; 2
survives on movement.
