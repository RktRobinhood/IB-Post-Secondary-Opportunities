# Austria degree photos: photo editor, round 1, 5/10 (30 Sep 2026)

I judged `contact-sheet-round-1.jpg` (22 photos). I checked 18 of them at 720 px in
`src/assets/img/programmes/school-at-*-720.webp`, including every card the researcher
flagged, and read every line in `docs/research/programme-images/schools-at.jsonl`. I
compared the set with the accepted German (`../de/contact-sheet-round-2.jpg`), Swedish
(`../se/contact-sheet-round-2.jpg`) and Belgian round-1 sets.

## Score: 5/10

Austria has some of the best single frames in the catalogue:

- 4, grapes tipping into the bin at harvest, which is right for Krems;
- 7, a DNA gel glowing under UV;
- 11, a guide leading a roped group through blue glacier ice;
- 15, a concierge on the phone with a room key;
- 21, two readers over a poem on a sunny bench.

But eight of the 22 fail, and the failures are not about polish:

- **Four repeat a subject another country already shows:** camera crew, bioreactor,
  podcast mic, EEG cap.
- **Three are black-and-white archive photos** that make a modern degree look like a
  museum.
- **One shows an exhibition's visitors** instead of the work.

**With the 8 rejects below removed, the remaining 14 score 8/10.** This is only just
an 8: half of the 14 carry a reservation.

## Flagged but kept

- **2** `school-at-aau-information-and-communications-engineering` (the letters "BG" on
  the T-shirt): **keep with reservation.** Two letters at the top edge do not fight a
  title set at the bottom, and there is no brand to read at 480 px. The mat also carries
  a faint maker's script, bottom left, which is illegible at card size. Soldering is a
  strong, textless subject that no other country shows. If the file is ever re-cut,
  dropping the top tenth removes both marks.

## Keep with reservations

These are the first to replace when a better photo turns up. None blocks shipping.

- **5** `school-at-imc-krems-chemistry`: a vivid, retro-colour synthesis rig. It is close in subject to Germany's medicinal chemistry card (DE #10, a woman at a glassware rig). It stays because the goggled face and the flask make it a different picture.
- **10** `school-at-imc-krems-tourism-and-leisure-management`: a guide with a group outside an abbey. It is the second guide-and-group card in Austria (with 11) and repeats Belgium's cave tour (BE #17) in idea. Faces are identifiable. The abbey (Rolduc) is in the Netherlands.
- **13** `school-at-modul-bsc-in-human-resources-management`: a posed stock interview at a glass table, all beige. It shows the discipline, but no one would stop scrolling for it.
- **16** `school-at-modul-bba-in-tourism-hotel-management-and-operations`: a 1920s pastry kitchen in black and white. The kitchen still reads, so it stays, but it must be the country's only archive photo.
- **17** `school-at-webster-vienna-comparative-international-business-law`: barristers in wigs at a mock trial. The subject is right, but it is a busy flash snapshot with faces as the subject.
- **19** `school-at-aau-social-sciences`: an oral-history recording in a cluttered garage. It is honest fieldwork but visually chaotic, and a red-label drink bottle sits at the left of the table (illegible at 480 px).

## Directions for replacements (candidates, not prescriptions)

- **1 Digital Media, Culture & Communication:** no camera crew, mic or podcast. Options:
  hands editing on a physical video mixer or grading desk (no legible screen text), or a
  projection-mapping install on a building at night. Klagenfurt or any Austrian city
  would be ideal.
- **6 Medical & Pharmaceutical Biotechnology:** out of the wet lab. Options: a
  cleanroom vial-filling line with gowned hands, or tablets on a pharmaceutical press.
  No bioreactors, pipettes or hoods.
- **8 Chemistry & Chemical Technology:** a modern polymer or process subject in colour:
  a plastics extruder with the melt strand coming out, or a pilot-plant column with an
  operator. No scan borders; check the edges of every archive scan.
- **12 Transformation Studies (Art x Science):** the maker, not the visitor. An artist
  building a kinetic or scientific installation, or hands working on a bio-art or
  light piece. Ars Electronica in Linz would be ideal if Commons has a textless frame.
- **14 International Relations:** no chambers (Sweden already has two) and no archive.
  A negotiation table seen wide with small figures and no emblems. Vienna's UN City
  exterior is not a discipline; avoid it.
- **18 Strategic Communication:** not a mic or a camera. Options: a campaign team
  arranging poster proofs on a wall (textless at 480 px), or a press conference podium
  from behind.
- **20 Informatics:** not a code screen, rack or 1960s room. Options: hands assembling
  a single-board-computer cluster, a robot or drone under test on a bench, or a sensor
  network being installed outdoors.
- **22 Psychology:** not an EEG cap. Options: an eye-tracking set-up seen from behind,
  an observation room through a one-way mirror (adults only, faces small), or a reaction-time
  experiment with hands on a response box.

## Light and dark

14 and 18 were the darkest and are both rejected. 8 and 20 were grey, low-contrast
frames that would go muddy under the veil, and are also rejected. 9 is dark but lit
from inside by the green beam, and it survives in both themes.
