## Entry, language, money and application: online BA

The programme gives upper-secondary access equivalent to UK Level 3, no specific
subjects, and individual assessment of other qualifications. An online meeting is
offered; it is not labelled a compulsory admission interview. Relevant work is
considered; weights, capacity and cutoff are unpublished.

The [live IBA application form](https://www.service4mobility.com/europe/BewerbungServlet?aust_prog=E-BA_IBM&identifier=KOLDING10&kz_bew_art=IN&kz_bew_pers=S&sprache=en)
is specifically `E-BA_IBM`; higher education is optional. It offers an English-taught
IB Diploma as language proof, asks for IELTS Academic 6.0, but lists TOEFL iBT **65**.
The programme page instead specifies TOEFL iBT **79**, minimum **19** per section.
Keep that conflict; do not silently choose 65. The form also lists CAE 169, CPE
pass, FCE 169, PTE 52, and Oxford 70 restricted to EU/EEA/Swiss. These form options
do not remove the programme's differing thresholds or its LBU-regulated exemptions.
The form was only read; no personal data, application, payment or consent was sent.

[International Qualifications](https://iba.dk/international/international-qualifications/)
is explicitly guidance for **Coventry courses**, gives IB undergraduate **24 points**
and an English-medium waiver, and reserves individual/course-specific assessment.
Do not import that 24-point threshold into the Leeds Beckett BA. No reviewed source
states an IBA/LBU **DP Course Results** acceptance rule; no automatic national AP
Course Results rule is applicable to a UK BA merely because IBA is a Danish business
academy. That award route remains **unresolved, not rejected**.

The [dedicated English page](https://iba.dk/international/english-language-requirements/)
is stamped March 2022 and covers Coventry courses. Its programme list includes the
new BSc IBM, but the table headings omit it. It restricts IB-in-lieu-of-IELTS to
EU/EEA/Swiss and gives English A HL/SL 5 or English B HL 5 / SL 7 in its undergraduate
column. This conflicts with the broad Coventry English-medium guidance and cannot
be transferred to the online LBU BA. Do not claim a universal IB waiver.

The online BA asks applicants not yet graduated for detailed transcripts and an
expected graduation date; diploma/transcript, English proof, passport/ID and payment
receipt are required. No separate predicted-grade form, final IB delivery mechanism,
May-session final-result deadline, reply date or offer-acceptance date is published.
These are explicit questions to agree with IBA, not inferred permission for late
results. The 22 August 2027 document/application date is published and remains useful.

Online BA pricing is **DKK 155,000 total**: registration 10,000, twenty modules at
6,500, research project 15,000; units are paid as they start. No citizenship tuition
exemption is stated; it is not ordinary free Danish public-degree tuition. No
scholarships or SU are offered. Its English exemptions for previous English-medium
education are subject to LBU regulations, not automatic Diploma/Course Results rules.

The [application-fee page](https://iba.dk/international/application-fee/), updated
June 2026 and with 2027 autumn payment links, explicitly lists the online BA:
**DKK 750 plus service charge for every citizenship**, exempting IBA graduates
applying to another programme. Payment must arrive by the application deadline;
the fee is non-refundable and not deducted from tuition. The service charge amount
is unpublished here. Do not equate registration with this separate application fee.
