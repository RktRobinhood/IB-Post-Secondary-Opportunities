## Identity, recognition and duplicate reconciliation

equates Funen Art Academy with Det Fynske Kunstakademi. It describes an
independent state-recognised institution financed with municipal and Ministry
of Culture support, founded in 1944. Its current location is **Jernbanegade
13, 5000 Odense C**, in the former Funen Museum of Art; Brandts was its
earlier location through 2018. The existing Place `dk-odense` is reused.
No additional current campus is established.

award, prove current institutional accreditation, or promise an individual

[13 May 2015 ministerial answer](https://www.ft.dk/samling/20141/almdel/fiv/spm/219/svar/1251971/1529390/index.htm)
also distinguishes the independent academy's framework from the state Royal
Academy's statutory obligations. It is historical context only, not a current
accreditation decision.
