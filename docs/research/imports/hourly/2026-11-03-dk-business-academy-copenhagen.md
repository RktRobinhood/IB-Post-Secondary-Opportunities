institution-owned language declaration above carries the exclusion decision.

### Why the supplied programme lead does not qualify

The [supplied Creative Branding catalogue page](https://studyindenmark.dk/portal/business-academy-copenhagen/ek-guldbergsgade/creative-branding-design-and-business)
labels the item 30 ECTS and one semester, despite its generic Bachelor heading.
It links directly to the institution's
[Creative Branding exchange page](https://www.ek.dk/english/exchange/creative-branding-design-and-business).
The official page requires at least two completed years of study in a related
field and nomination by a partner home university. It advertises **spring
2027**, with a **1 November 2026** application deadline. Neither that intake
nor that deadline belongs in the autumn 2027 first-year application calendar.

That exchange page describes two 15-ECTS modules, group projects and company
cases, and B2 English (TOEFL 550 / IELTS 6.0). Those English-language details
belong to exchange applicants; they are not evidence of an IB school-leaver
degree route or an IB English waiver.

The [Danish Creative Branding programme page](https://www.ek.dk/videregaaende-uddannelser/creative-branding-design-og-business)
instead describes the 1.5-year, 90-ECTS professional-bachelor top-up at
Guldbergsgade, requiring an eligible AP qualification and an admissions test.
Its last advertised intake is summer 2027. This helps distinguish the full
top-up from the exchange semester; it does not create an English first-year
opportunity. Its application and selection details were not imported into the
first-year product.
