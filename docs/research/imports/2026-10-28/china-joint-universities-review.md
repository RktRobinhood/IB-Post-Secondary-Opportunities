# Five joint-university records

Reviewed 28 October 2026. These are curated derivatives of the archived returns,
not blanket approval of every returned claim. All five use the existing catalogue
template, with eleven institution-backed programme examples and faculty guidance.
The full course catalogue remains the handoff. Original packs are unchanged.

Official HTML was read through the web reader and direct requests. Where the
Python TLS handshake failed for CUHK-Shenzhen and WKU, Windows `curl.exe` retrieved
the actual official pages successfully. This resolved access failures without
using search snippets as evidence. Programme descriptions were checked against
the published programme pages; fields unsupported for the requested cycle remain
absent or explicitly qualified. Photos are a separate pending parity layer.

## CUHK-Shenzhen

The [2027 requirements](https://intladmissions.cuhk.edu.cn/en/taxonomy/term/149)
explicitly give the full IB Diploma at 30/45, relevant subject preparation and
IB English grade 4 at HL or SL as an English-evidence route. That general score
does not replace Financial Engineering's supplementary requirements. No invented
AA/AI, HL/SL or subject-grade equivalence was added.

The [actual rounds page](https://intladmissions.cuhk.edu.cn/en/page/190) confirms
1 November 2026, 7 March 2027 and 18 April 2027, all at 23:59:59 China time.
The last round makes no conditional offers, so it is marked for already-qualified
applicants rather than presented as a May-2027 IB student's deadline. English
evidence has its separate 28 May deadline. The [fee table](https://www.cuhk.edu.cn/en/node/8803)
was inspected as a table: **undergraduate** CNY162,000 per academic year applies
to 2027 entrants, distinct from earlier cohorts and postgraduate charges.

Financial Engineering and Artificial Intelligence are the selected examples:
the [three-school finance curriculum](https://sme.cuhk.edu.cn/en/node/3397) and
[dedicated interdisciplinary AI programme](https://sai.cuhk.edu.cn/en/page/47)
provide the rationale. The pack's uncertain Computer Science award title and
Clinical Medicine's unread supplementary schedule were not guessed into public
programme records. Both remain discoverable through the school's catalogue and
faculty guidance.

Next check **2026-11-04**: programme-specific IB subjects/grades, Clinical Medicine
international and clinical-language conditions, confirmed first-year music
selection, offer/final-IB milestones and award-specific scholarship conditions.

## Duke Kunshan

The [2027 checklist](https://admissions.dukekunshan.edu.cn/en/how-to-apply/) confirms
binding Early Decision on 2 November 2026 and Regular Decision on 4 January 2027,
both 23:59 US Eastern time. Standardised and English tests are optional, not
invented compulsory minima. Need-based aid must be requested during admission;
an admitted international student who did not request it cannot apply later.

Students enter a common first year and declare majors later. The [major catalogue](https://ugstudies.dukekunshan.edu.cn/academics/majors/)
is the handoff. Environmental Science and Global Health are supported by the
university's [corresponding research centres](https://www.dukekunshan.edu.cn/about/academics/)
and their own curricula. Generic dual bachelor's credentials preserve the two
awarding institutions without inventing a single award abbreviation for every
track. The pack's Data Science research-centre justification was not carried over
unchanged: the current institutional research-centre list differs.

The [tuition page](https://admissions.dukekunshan.edu.cn/en/tuition-international-students/)
still identifies 2026/27. Its USD73,740 tuition/fees and USD84,065 estimated total
are historical, not 2027/28 prices. The [scholarship-specific page](https://admissions.dukekunshan.edu.cn/en/duke-kunshan-university-full-scholarship/)
and FAQ give inconsistent CSS dates; funding deadlines were not turned into
admission closure. Next check **2026-11-02**: reconcile financial-aid instructions
before Early Decision; then exact final-result, reply, deposit and 2027/28 fees.

## Nottingham Ningbo

The [entry page](https://www.nottingham.edu.cn/en/study-with-us/undergraduate/entry-requirements/)
accepts the full IB Diploma for the qualifying year, leaving three years of study.
The 28–34 range is a typical offer range with course-specific HL conditions, not
a universal floor. Aerospace's central qualifying-year IELTS requirement is 6.5
with every element 6.0; lower programme-prospectus figures remain a conflict.

The [current application page](https://www.nottingham.edu.cn/en/study-with-us/undergraduate/how-to-apply.aspx)
explicitly confirms **30 June 2027** application closure and **15 July 2027**
conditional results/English documents. Its exception for qualifications released
later is not a blanket IB extension. The [scholarship page](https://www.nottingham.edu.cn/en/study-with-us/undergraduate/fees-and-scholarships/international.aspx)
separately confirms 31 May 2027 for named international scholarship applications.
The Finance/Accounting/Management course's national recognition and professional
links and the engineering faculty's accreditation support the two examples.

Next check **2026-11-04**: exact course-level IB subjects and grades, resolution
of Aerospace English conflicts, the applicable 2027 tuition invoice and final
IB certificate arrangements. No 28-point `points` value was assigned to either
programme.

## Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool

The [requirements](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/admissions/ug/global/entry-requirements)
distinguish Year One from Year Two. A full IB Diploma with 30 points permits
Year Two consideration, with course prerequisites; it is not a Year One minimum.
Engineering/Industrial Design require Mathematics and Physics; Economics/Finance
require Mathematics. No AA/AI, level or grade was inferred. The records distinguish
the normal four years from eligible IB holders' three remaining years.

The [application page](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/admissions/global/how-to-apply)
now confirms **31 May 2027 for most undergraduate programmes**; the 30 April
transfer deadline is excluded. The [current fee/funding page](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/admissions/global/fees-and-scholarships/)
posts CNY99,000 annually, a 31 March 2027 early-bird application/offer condition,
separate scholarship rounds and exceptions for later IB/A-level final results.
Its nationality and under-age conditions were retained without assuming that
school attendance in Denmark determines international status.

Robotics, Industrial Design and Economics/Finance are supported by their schools'
industry partnerships, design research and business accreditation respectively.
Next check **2026-11-04**: course-specific exceptions to the general deadline,
IB subject/English equivalences, final-result/deposit milestones and precise
scholarship/document conditions. Fees are labelled as published rather than an
unconditional promise about every entrant's final invoice.
