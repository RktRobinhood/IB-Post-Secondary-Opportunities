# Six-institution repair and integration review

Reviewed 28 October 2026 against the supplied packs and live official pages. All six
institutions have school records. This is an integration with named remaining gaps,
not a declaration that every 2027 rule has been published or independently signed off.
The original packs remain evidence of what was returned, including their errors.

## American University of Armenia

Integrated eight four-year school-leaver degrees; Nursing remains excluded because
it requires an earlier nursing or midwifery diploma. The [undergraduate directory](https://admissions.aua.am/ugrad/)
and [FAQ](https://admissions.aua.am/ugrad/faqs/) confirm programme scope, English
instruction and that international non-speakers need not take Armenian-taught courses.
The guessed selection URL in the pilot record was replaced with the actual
[selection page](https://admissions.aua.am/ugrad/selection/).

The directory now explicitly identifies Fall 2027: applications open 1 November 2026;
early admission is 15 January, regular admission 30 April, and rolling admission
1 June 2027. Rolling availability depends on capacity, so its date is provisional
for any particular programme. This resolves the pack's earlier year-label conflict.
The [current tuition page](https://admissions.aua.am/ugrad/tuition-and-fees/) also
explicitly identifies Fall 2027: international annual tuition is AMD 4,950,000 for
Business/Economics and AMD 4,450,000 for the other six degrees. The displayed rates
are before financial aid. Armenian citizens, documented ethnic Armenians and Special
Passport holders can qualify for different rates. The page separately lists a
AMD 25,000 services fee and requires non-Armenian citizens to hold health insurance;
its approximately AMD 10,000 monthly insurance figure is an optional provider estimate,
not a compulsory insurance premium payable to AUA.

The [international scholarship route](https://finaid.aua.am/international-scholarships/)
is competitive and can cover up to 50% of published international tuition.
The [financial aid calendar](https://finaid.aua.am/deadlines/) now gives
30 June 2027 for international scholarship applications; its 19 January, 4 May and
2 June dates are separately labelled undergraduate need-based tuition assistance,
not general admission deadlines. The tuition page says to apply for aid alongside
admission, so applicants should establish the applicable submission procedure rather
than assume June 30 permits waiting until after admission. These funding events
were retained here instead of being mislabelled as application closure in a schema
without a scholarship milestone type.

The [requirements](https://admissions.aua.am/ugrad/application-requirements/) and
selection sources establish school records, English evidence and maths examination
evidence/approved waivers. IB maths waiver examples within transfer-applicant guidance
were not imported as first-year subject prerequisites. No invented IB points floor
or conversion from SAT/ACT was added. The central selection table and ESS-specific
material require another check for consistent effective thresholds before publishing
an exact ESS rule. Precise final-IB submission dates and offer/deposit milestones
remain unestablished. No capacity or historical lowest-admitted IB cutoff was found.

Next check: **2026-11-02**, especially the newly opening Fall 2027 application and
programme screening tables.

## ADA University

Integrated the eighteen predominantly English degree options in the
[current course finder](https://www.ada.edu.az/en/admission/find-your-program), with
the local-language condition in the school summary and **every programme's IB line**.
The repo's school brief permits a programme with a local-language requirement when
that requirement is explicit. This record does not say non-speakers can complete
these degrees. The largely bilingual Law curriculum is excluded from this English
primary list, rather than presenting its twenty-four English courses as an entirely
English LLB.

The [May 2025 Foundation regulations](https://www.ada.edu.az/media/2025/07/31/_02_00_approved_foundation_program_regulations.pdf),
page 2, require two Azerbaijani-taught core courses, while allowing documented
programme adaptations. The [current Foundation page](https://www.ada.edu.az/en/schools/school-chs/foundation-program)
lists Azerbaijani Studies but does not establish a non-speaker exemption.
The [Agricultural Technologies curriculum](https://www.ada.edu.az/en/schools/safs/programs/agricultural-technologies)
also lists Azerbaijani writing in its major core. **Original pack c008 cites the wrong
source, s20 (International Studies); the relevant source is s31 (Agricultural
Technologies).** The integrated record uses the correct URL.

The [undergraduate admission page](https://www.ada.edu.az/en/admission/undergraduate)
separates international applicants from Azerbaijani citizens and specified residents/
dual citizens. The 25-point IB route is in the latter section, so it is not a generic
international admission minimum. International school records, an English interview
for shortlisted candidates, and the ADA plus Study in Azerbaijan application stages
are recorded. The current dates remain 2026; no one-year projection was made.

The pack's fee conflicts and 2025 price table do not establish 2027 charges. The
Topchubashov fellowship is an official international funding route, but a current
award decision, amount or 2027 deadline has not been established. IB Course Results,
international IB subjects/waivers, programme-specific portfolios, capacities and
historical admitted-score cutoffs remain unestablished.

Next check: **2026-11-04** for Fall 2027 admission/fees and a documented international
adaptation of the Azerbaijani component. Absence of an exemption is not evidence that
all international students are rejected.

## Euro University of Bahrain

Integrated ten published paths grouped into **three programme cards**: Business
Administration, Computer Science and Law. The [University of London teaching-centre
entry](https://www.london.ac.uk/study/where-study/teaching-centre/bahrain/euro-university-bahrain)
expressly identifies the three Business specialisms; EUB's [academic programmes
FAQ](https://eub.edu.bh/academic-programmes/) identifies the four Computer Science
specialisms. Every path links to its own existing EUB programme page. They are not
presented as ten unrelated degree families. The standalone IFP is excluded.
The four computing paths absent as separate objects from the submitted pack now have
sourced descriptions, three-year direct-entry duration and their own URLs.

The [direct-entry page](https://eub.edu.bh/general-entry/direct-entry/) gives a
standing 28-point IB rule. Its TOEFL 85 conflicts with the academic FAQ's TOEFL 87;
individual programme pages repeat lower IELTS/TOEFL figures resembling foundation
entry. The public record therefore identifies the conflict and does not select the
lowest score. It does not assert that the standing page is a confirmed 2027 policy.
Neither an IB English waiver nor an IB subject-level conversion was established.

University of London requires engagement/enrolment with both the centre and the
awarding university. The net invoice must not be inferred by adding or subtracting
figures from different fee pages. Available subject fee PDFs are 2025/26, and the
general advertised semester price lacks a 2027 applicability label. 2027 tuition,
mandatory extras, deadlines, scholarships, programme-specific ranking and final-IB
handling remain to be confirmed. No historical admitted-score cutoff or capacity
was located; admission eligibility is not admission probability.

Next check: **2026-11-04** for 2027/28 fees, dates and reconciled direct-entry English
requirements.

## RCSI Bahrain

Integrated Medicine and Nursing with their descriptions and distinct selection
routes. Medicine is recorded as the five-year core, with its possible additional
Foundation Year explicit in the description and school note, not as an unrelated
foundation-only degree. The [current Medicine requirements](https://www.rcsi.com/bahrain/undergraduate/medicine/entry-requirements)
now directly show **36/45 for five-year entry and 31/45 for six-year entry** including
core points. This supersedes the pack's source-reader observation of 35/30. However,
the page still carries a September 2026 banner, so the points remain qualified text
pending explicit 2027 confirmation rather than unqualified `points` fields. Its two
explicitly dated Casper sessions, 17 January and 12 February 2027, are medicine-only
test milestones, not application deadlines.

The [Nursing requirements](https://www.rcsi.com/bahrain/undergraduate/undergraduate-nursing/entry-requirements)
show September 2027 and closed admissions while the rule body still says 2026/27.
That status is prominent; no invented future application window or assumption that
the programme is open was added. Current subject rules and Aptis arrangements need
2027 applicability checked with the responsible source before unqualified encoding.

The [Medicine fee page](https://www.rcsi.com/bahrain/undergraduate/medicine/fees)
still labels its non-Bahraini USD 49,400 annual charge and USD 10,000 acceptance
deposit as 2026/27. The [Nursing fee page](https://www.rcsi.com/bahrain/undergraduate/undergraduate-nursing/fees)
likewise labels USD 15,100 a year and USD 3,000 deposit as 2026/27. These amounts were
not published as 2027 tuition. The [HEC instructions](https://www.rcsi.com/bahrain/undergraduate/application-information/higher-education-council-requirements)
require school-document authentication and subsequent HEC registration confirmation;
alternative submission dates can be arranged where original certificates are delayed.
This is not a universal dated extension. Course Results policy, 2027 fees/funding,
intake windows and exact final-result/reply milestones remain open.

Next check: **2026-11-02**, prioritising the mixed intake labels and Nursing closure.

Integrated the large English-medium catalogue handoff, seven faculties and one
source-supported Law flagship. The [Faculty of Law](https://www.cavehill.uwi.edu/law/)
expressly presents regional legal-education strength and history, while the
[LLB page](https://www.cavehill.uwi.edu/law/programme/ll-b-degree-bachelor-of-laws/)

applicants. The other three pack choices were editorial samples without an equivalent

Further flagship enrichment remains a recorded gap.

The [2027/28 admission calendar](https://www.cavehill.uwi.edu/admissions/application-process/deadline-dates/)
now explicitly says general admission dates are to be determined. Its April 15 exchange
date and May 31 specially admitted date are **not** full-degree first-year closing
dates. The current [entry guide](https://www.cavehill.uwi.edu/admissions/wp-content/uploads/sites/98/UWI_Cave_Hill_UG_Entry_Requirements_2026_27.pdf)
is still for 2026/27. Its 24/45 IB rule is labelled historical in the public summary,
not assigned to every competitive programme as a current points floor.

The [financial aid programme](https://www.cavehill.uwi.edu/studentfinancing/financial-aid/financial-aid-programme/)
is for continuing CARICOM undergraduates and explicitly excludes first-year students;
it is not an available first-year Danish-school funding promise. Nationality
restrictions on scholarships require award-specific checking. 2027 tuition, final IB
milestones, English waivers, subject equivalences, programme capacity and cutoffs
remain unestablished.
