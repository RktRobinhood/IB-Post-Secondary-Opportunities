# NYU Shanghai and BNBU integration review

Source cycle: 28 October 2026. Two catalogue school records were created, each with
two institution-supported flagship choices and guidance for its undergraduate
academic units. Original packs remain unchanged in `returned/`. The coordinator's
official-page cache was read, with additional live web and native-curl checks for

## NYU Shanghai

Integrated the [nineteen-major catalogue](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/content/majors),
shared four-year first-year entry, three academic divisions, IB/predicted-results
guidance, English-medium schooling waiver, Chinese-language study and the
international Common Application route. The [application checklist](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/page/how-apply)
does not publish an IB points floor or major-specific IB grade thresholds.
Chinese citizens use a separate admissions process; a Danish school address does
not establish international-route eligibility. [Undergraduate study](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/undergraduate)
includes study at another NYU location, rather than requiring a separate transfer
application for a second first degree.

The pack's four representative majors did not by themselves establish flagship
status. Two were selected after checking Shanghai-specific institutional strengths:

- Data Science: the [Shanghai CSDSE division](https://datascience.shanghai.nyu.edu/about-us)
  describes its research, practical undergraduate education, facilities and industry
  relationships. This is Shanghai evidence, not the similarly named New York centre.
  The [BS curriculum](https://bulletins.nyu.edu/undergraduate/shanghai/programs/data-science-bs/)
  confirms programming, probability/statistics, linear algebra, machine learning,
  databases, concentrations and the capstone structure.
- Neural Science: the [NYU-ECNU Institute in Shanghai](https://bcs.shanghai.nyu.edu/en/about-us/vision)
  explicitly joins interdisciplinary brain research with undergraduate training.
  The [BS curriculum](https://bulletins.nyu.edu/undergraduate/shanghai/programs/neural-science-bs/)
  supports the description and honours pathway. Post-enrolment major requirements
  were not misrepresented as first-year IB prerequisites.

The Shanghai admissions page lists November 1, January 1 and January 5 without a
cycle year. The record keeps these as **provisional yearly dates** for the next
intake, with Early Decision labelled as binding rounds. A New York artistic-review
page was not used to certify Shanghai's 2027 rules. The shared university admissions
site is behind a browser challenge on this check. Exact cycle confirmation therefore
remains open instead of being silently asserted.

The [Shanghai funding page](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/page/how-apply-financial-aid)
now explicitly requests the Fall 2027/Spring 2028 CSS Profile. Its financial-aid
deadlines are November 10, 2026 for ED I, January 10, 2027 for ED II, and February 1,
2027 for Regular Decision, all by 11:59 p.m. Eastern Standard Time. These are stated
in the school note and kept separate from application closure. Non-Chinese students
seeking institutional aid apply before admission; applicable noncustodial-parent
information or a waiver is necessary. The institution generally renews the same
grant amount rather than reassessing need annually. FAFSA is only for eligible US
federal-aid applicants, not automatically for EU/EEA students.

The [preparation page](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/page/secondary-school-preparation)
asks for proof of graduation by July 1 of enrolment year, with specified possible
international calendar extensions. The application checklist asks for the final
report showing graduation by August 1. They may represent distinct document stages,
but this does not justify treating August 1 as a blanket extension of July 1. The
record names both and withholds an unconditional final-document calendar entry.
New admitted-class instructions should settle the applicable case. Predicted IB
grades must come from a school official and final scores are still required.

The [international tuition schedule](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/cn/node/27920) remains
2026/27: CNY 231,437 per semester for 12-18 credits, with insurance, housing and other
charges separately listed. It is not a confirmed Fall 2027 price. The distinct US
billing category was not substituted for the non-US international category. The
school schema cannot express all fee groups, grant conditions or dated funding
milestones, so this detail is retained in the review instead of squeezing it into
an unqualified tuition field. No international programme capacities or historical
lowest-admitted IB cutoffs were established.

Next check: **2026-10-30** for imminent ED I cycle confirmation and new admitted-class
document guidance; check 2027/28 tuition when the next schedule is published.

## Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University, formerly UIC

Integrated the renamed institution under its existing `cn-uic-zhuhai` identity.
The [current international catalogue](https://admission.bnbu.edu.cn/ido/programmes_courses.htm)
now lists **32** programmes across four undergraduate faculty/school headings,
replacing the pack's count of thirty. It is an English-medium catalogue with
[explicit bilingual exceptions](https://ar.bnbu.edu.cn/quality_assurance/College_Policies_and_Guidelines/Medium_of_Instruction.htm)
for Whole Person Education experiential modules and Healthy Lifestyle; no unsupported
promise of entirely English delivery was made.

The [application page](https://admission.bnbu.edu.cn/ido/how_to_apply.htm) now gives
**May 15, 2027** explicitly. This resolves the pack's older/undated deadline gap;
the school record carries a confirmed international first-year closing date.
The page identifies the current international portal. An older FAQ still uses the
2026 cycle and an earlier portal URL, so the current how-to-apply route was preferred.

The [international qualification table](https://admission.bnbu.edu.cn/ido/Admission_Requirements/international_curricula.htm)
accepts the IB Diploma without publishing an overall IB points minimum. The
[English page](https://admission.bnbu.edu.cn/ido/Admission_Requirements/english_language_requirements.htm)
accepts English A at grade 4 HL/SL, English B at 4 HL or 5 SL, and Literature and
Performance at 4 SL. Alternatives include IELTS Academic 6.0 and TOEFL iBT 79. Its
test-validity rule explicitly applies from 2027: for that entry, qualifying tests
must be from September 1, 2025 onward. The page labels the requirements as proposed;
applicants should check for final revisions. No Diploma-course combination or
subject-specific equivalence was invented.

The two selected flagship examples now have a stronger institutional basis:

- [Game Design](https://scc.bnbu.edu.cn/gd_en/index.htm): native curl opened the newly
  redesigned official page after the source reader timed out. The page presents the
  programme as mainland China's first undergraduate Game Design offering, identifies
  its 2025 first cohort and dedicated production facilities. The description omits
  the marketing ranking claim while the source explains the flagship choice.
  [The actual curriculum URL](https://scc.bnbu.edu.cn/gd_en/curriculum_en.htm) gives a
  four-year 2026-cohort study plan and 148 units; the pack's `curriculum.htm` URL is
  obsolete. This replaces its much older general-duration evidence.
- [Globalisation and Development](https://fhss.bnbu.edu.cn/gad_en/): the programme
  describes its interdisciplinary social-science/STEM approach and dedicated
  GAD-CBCGDF sustainability-leadership cooperation. Its [curriculum](https://fhss.bnbu.edu.cn/gad_en/curriculum.htm)
  gives research, digital analysis, sustainability and the two later concentrations.
  These are specific institutional features rather than an invented rank.

The [Academic Registry degree-title table](https://ar.bnbu.edu.cn/current_students/student_handbook/each.htm)
confirms the Creative Arts honours and Social Sciences honours awards. The
[admissions FAQ](https://admission.bnbu.edu.cn/ido/faqs.htm) says shortlisted
international applicants normally interview, with arrangements varying by programme.
It does not justify importing domestic broad-category major-allocation rules as
universal international rules. Additional 2027 Game Design portfolio/subject steps
remain to be confirmed.

The [fee page](https://admission.bnbu.edu.cn/ido/tuition_and_expenses.htm) currently
advertises RMB 110,000 annual tuition, or RMB 132,000 for Music Performance, without
a 2027 tuition label. It lists accommodation separately and marks insurance
RMB 600 and utilities prepayment RMB 800 as 2026 standards. These were not promoted
to confirmed 2027 charges. The [scholarship policy](https://admission.bnbu.edu.cn/ido/scholarship.htm)
is expressly for 2026 entrants: IB 36+ for its full award, 33-35 for 50%, and 30-32
for 30% tuition awards are scholarship criteria, not entry cutoffs. Selection is
competitive, continued awards normally require cGPA 3.0, and housing waivers are
limited. None is encoded as a 2027 entitlement or an admission probability.

Next check: **2026-11-04** for final 2027 English wording, tuition/scholarships,
programme-specific selection and final-IB/offer milestones. A May 2027 application
deadline is established; no confirmed 2027 class-start, offer-response, deposit or
final-IB-results deadline was found.
