# Denmark — university colleges, business academies and the art academy added as canonical records

Started: 2026-09-24. Intake: 2027-autumn. Scope and exclusions come from DK_COVERAGE.md, Task 2.
Each programme was re-read on its official page on 2026-09-24 before a record was written. When
a page needs a browser to load its content, the entry says so.

The existing university records all carry an entry-award rule (which IB award opens the programme).
For every programme below, that rule comes from the national rule in `ev-ufsn-ib-course-results`.
The Agency says DP Course Results of at least 18 points, with grade 3 or more in all six subjects and
three at HL, give access to erhvervsakademi (AP) and professionsbachelor (PBA) programmes through
quota 1 and quota 2. No institution page below mentions Course Results. For the BEng (diplomingeniør)
programmes at VIA and Absalon, the rule applies because both institutions describe them as
professional bachelor's degrees. The records cite that statement next to the national rule.

## Exclusions confirmed on re-check

- VIA, Software Technology Engineering, Cybersecurity specialisation (Horsens): February 2028 start only
  ("Apply in 2027 for study start in February 2028"). It has no autumn 2027 intake, so it is not added.
  https://en.via.dk/programmes/bachelor/software-cybersecurity
- Absalon, Professional Bachelor's Degree in Education (Vordingborg): "taught partly in English and partly in
  Danish". Not added. https://en.phabsalon.dk/full-degree/professional-bachelors-degree-education
- VIA, Design and Business top-up (Herning), BAAA Digital Concept Development top-up, and SEA Software
  Development and Web Development (professionsbachelor top-ups): these need a prior AP degree, so they are out of scope.
- VIA's Branding and Marketing Management and Fashion Design are specialisations of the Design, Technology and
  Business AP degree, not separate programmes. They are recorded as one programme.

## Requirements not published, or not published in full

- Absalon Biotechnology BEng: neither the English nor the Danish pages give the programme's length, so no duration is recorded.
- Absalon Robot Systems BEng: Absalon says the assessment criteria "have not yet been finalised". The selection record says that, and the subject requirements are recorded as published.
- SEA (Computer Science, Multimedia Design): no tuition statement on the pages read, so no cost is recorded.
- Dania Cyber Security: non-EU tuition "will follow".
- Zealand (both): the IB line names "Mathematical Studies, standard level (SL)", a course the IB replaced in 2019. It is recorded as Mathematics AI or AA at SL or higher, and the note says so.
- None of the institutions publishes a grade-average floor or a 2026 cut-off on the pages read. The national KOT 2026 list (ev-ufsn-kot-hovedtal-2026) carries cut-offs for some of these programmes (for example 41165 VIA ATCM and 48505 Absalon Biotechnology), but its wrapped PDF table could not be transcribed reliably, so no historicalCutoffs were recorded.
