# Where IB students go that this site does not list yet

From the IB Recognition Statements Database, read 2026-09-23 (see
[IB_STATEMENTS.md](../IB_STATEMENTS.md)). **387** universities in our 35
Destinations, outside the US, have an IB statement, teach full degrees in
English, and are not on the site. Ranked here by the one number that says
where IB students actually apply: **transcripts IB students sent in the last
five years.**

This is a shortlist for adding institutions — one line and a link each, the
same "touch" as everything else — not a list to import. Every addition still
has to meet the floor: a real institution, a link a student can act on.

## Read the numbers carefully

- A transcript **sent** is an application, not an admission.
- The count is worldwide and includes each country's own IB schools. The
  University of Tasmania's 6,313 almost certainly reflects Australian IB
  candidates, not students from Europe.
- Left out on purpose: pathway and foundation colleges (Kaplan, UNSW College,
  UTS College, Deakin College, Monash College, Fraser International College,
  the "International College" arms), HKU SPACE community colleges, and
  sub-faculties of institutions already listed (Erasmus School of Economics,
  Rotterdam School of Management — both inside Erasmus, which is on the site).
- Liberal-arts colleges inside a listed university **are** included, because
  they are a genuinely different thing to apply to: Amsterdam University
  College, University College Utrecht, University College Roosevelt.
