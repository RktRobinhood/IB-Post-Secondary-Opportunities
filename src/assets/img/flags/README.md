# Flags

One small SVG per Destination (`<code>.svg`, 3:2, 30 x 20 units), made by
`node scripts/make-flags.mjs`. Edit that script, not these files.

They replace the emoji flags on the globe, its cards and its list: Windows has
no flag emoji and draws the two letters instead ("GB United Kingdom"; the owner,
#53). Drawn for this site from each flag's published construction and
simplified to read at 18 x 12 CSS pixels (a coat of arms is a small shield,
Spain is its civil flag without arms). National flags are public-domain
designs; these drawings are the site's own. About 18 kB for all 36.
