# IB Post-Secondary Opportunities

This context describes how the product represents post-secondary choices for IB students and the evidence used to assess them.

## Study choices

**Opportunity**:
A specific programme offering at an institution for a particular intake, campus, and teaching language. It is the smallest useful item a student can assess or save.
_Avoid_: School, option, university

**Programme**:
A named course of study leading to a credential. A programme can produce multiple Opportunities when its intake, campus, language, or admissions rules differ.
_Avoid_: Course, major, degree

**Institution**:
The accredited post-secondary organization that owns or awards a Programme.
_Avoid_: School, university (unless the institution is specifically a university)

**Destination**:
A country or admissions jurisdiction whose rules, costs, and application process apply to Opportunities.
_Avoid_: Market, territory

**Intake**:
The dated admission cycle in which an Opportunity begins.
_Avoid_: Year, cohort

## Student planning

**Student Profile**:
The student's actual or predicted IB subjects, levels, grades, total points, citizenship or fee status, languages, interests, and planning preferences. Personally identifying information is not part of the profile.
_Avoid_: Applicant, account

**Eligibility Assessment**:
An explainable comparison of a Student Profile with the published Requirements for one Opportunity. It can establish requirement fit, not the probability of receiving an offer.
_Avoid_: Admission chance, acceptance prediction

**Requirement**:
A condition published for entry to an Opportunity, such as an IB total, subject, level, grade, language result, test, portfolio, interview, or activity.
_Avoid_: Preference, recommendation

**Selection Factor**:
Information used to rank otherwise eligible applicants, including grades, tests, essays, interviews, portfolios, or documented activities.
_Avoid_: Requirement

**Preparation Action**:
A concrete step a student can take before applying, such as choosing an IB subject, planning CAS evidence, sitting a test, building a portfolio, or researching an Extended Essay topic.
_Avoid_: Requirement (unless an official source makes it one)

**Exploration List**:
A student's reversible set of interesting Opportunities. Inclusion does not imply eligibility or an application decision.
_Avoid_: Shortlist, application list

## Applying

**Application Jurisdiction**:
The administrative area whose shared admissions rules or services apply to an Opportunity. It may be a country, province, state, territory, sector, or other authority boundary.
_Avoid_: Region, country

**Application System**:
A shared admissions process or portal used by multiple Institutions within an Application Jurisdiction. Its coverage may be comprehensive, partial, or optional.
_Avoid_: Website, national portal

**Application Route**:
The applicant-specific path used to apply for an Opportunity, including its Application System or direct submission, rounds, fees, Submission Items, and supplementary steps.
_Avoid_: Application System, application link

**Application Round**:
A named submission and decision window within an Application Route whose timing or rules affect consideration, priority, or outcome.
_Avoid_: Intake, deadline

**Application Milestone**:
A dated or time-bounded event in an Application Route, such as opening, submission, document, test, result, reply, deposit, visa, or enrolment activity.
_Avoid_: Deadline

**Deadline**:
An Application Milestone after which an action is no longer accepted or loses a stated form of consideration or priority.
_Avoid_: Date, target date

**Submission Item**:
A document, result, payment, choice, or applicant action that must be completed through an Application Route.
_Avoid_: Requirement, prerequisite

**Application Plan**:
A student's selected Opportunities, resolved Application Routes, checklist items, and Application Milestones. It is planning support, not an application submitted by this product.
_Avoid_: Exploration List, application

## Trust and evidence

**Evidence**:
A source-backed claim with a source URL, publisher, retrieval date, applicability period, and verification state.
_Avoid_: Note, citation

**Verification State**:
The product's confidence that an Evidence item is current and supports the displayed claim: verified, needs review, superseded, or unavailable.
_Avoid_: Valid, true

**Data Freshness**:
How recently Evidence was checked relative to the Intake and the source's expected change frequency. Freshness is not the same as correctness.
_Avoid_: Accuracy

**Official Source**:
A page or document published by the institution, admissions authority, government, qualification body, or application portal responsible for the rule.
_Avoid_: Primary source (when responsibility is unclear)

**Recognition Scheme**:
One Destination's published rules for reading an IB Diploma in its own vocabulary: the authority that publishes them, the subject scale and grade scale they are written on, and the tables that translate an IB subject and an IB grade into those scales. A Requirement written on a local scale names that scale and is assessed through the Scheme that defines it; a Requirement written in IB terms is assessed directly and needs no Scheme at all, which is why most Destinations have none.
_Avoid_: Conversion table, equivalence, mapping
